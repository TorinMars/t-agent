# Learn English

> Part of the T-Agent design document. The index of all parts is [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md).

## Learn English

Practice of the person's own English: the sentences they send with "Chinese → English, then send" and the words they look up with tap to translate become study cards, plus a starter word list. Progress is kept on the server, per owner, so the phone and the computer of the same person share it, and it is the same on every machine joined by service links (see "Sync across linked devices"); a machine marked as a company computer keeps only a cache (see "Company computer"). Meanings come from the translation service the person already set up; no other outside service is used.

- **Server**: `services/learn.js` (cards, queues, mistakes, summary, settings, meanings, starter import), `services/learn-review.js` (the review rules, pure; FSRS-6 in `services/learn-fsrs.js`, the replay of stored cards in `services/learn-schedule.js`, see [learn-review-fsrs.md](learn-review-fsrs.md)), `services/learn-starter.js` (the starter lists, English only), `routes/learn.js` (`/api/learn`). Tables `learn_cards` and `learn_reviews` in `db/schema.sql` (schema 13; the sync columns and `learn_card_moves` schema 14; `learn_cards.phonetic` schema 15; the detail of wrong answers and `learn_reports` schema 17). The mistake analysis: `services/learn-analysis.js` (see "Mistake reports"). The level and the words picked from translations: `services/learn-pick.js` (see "Your level" and "Picking words from translations"; the pick columns of `learn_cards` schema 19). The questions about a card: `services/learn-ask.js` (see "Ask about a question"; the table `learn_chats` schema 20). The English chat: `services/learn-chat.js` (the tables `learn_convos` and `learn_convo_messages` schema 21; see [learn-english-chat.md](learn-english-chat.md)). The phonetic transcription: `services/phonetics.js` with the data in `vendor/cmudict/` (see "Phonetic transcription"). - **Page**: `public/js/learn-collect.js` (the collection hooks, about 2 KB, a classic script combined with the others, not part of the MUI first screen); the Learn page itself is a lazy chunk of the MUI shell (`screen-learn.jsx`, with the grading in `learn-grade.mjs`).
- **Tests**: `test/phonetics.test.js` (ARPAbet to IPA, the data format on a fixture, known and unknown words, case, hyphenated words, possessives, the idle release, a missing file, the bundled file against its checksums), `test/learn-review.test.js` (the rules), `test/learn.test.js` (checks, routes, owners, meanings, starter, gates), the 12 → 13, 13 → 14 and 14 → 15 migrations and the refusal by an older app in `test/data-home.test.js`, `test/learn-mistakes.test.js` (the detail of a wrong answer, the Mistakes list, the analysis settings, the tag list on both sides, the question for the model, the check of its answer, the weekly rule and the weekly run with a fake clock, the routes with a stubbed model), `test/learn-pick.test.js` (the known rule, the questions for the level and the picks and the checks of their answers, picking with a stubbed model and a fake clock: new cards, existing cards, known words, the newest sentence, batches, a failure and the next run, a new level, the switch; ### Data

- `learn_cards`: `id`, `owner` (the page login, `req.session.user.login`), `kind` (`word` | `sentence`), `en`, `norm`, `zh` (`''` until a meaning is fetched), `phonetic` (schema 15: a word's IPA between slashes, `''` until known and always for a sentence), `source` (`translation` | `lookup` | `starter` | `manual`), `context` (where it came from, the task title, at most 120 characters; for a word asked inside one of the person's sentences that whole sentence, at most 300, see "Picking words from translations"), `created_at`, `due_at`, `interval_days` (real; whole days since schema 22), `ease` (2.5 to start; no longer used since schema 22, kept for older versions), `reps` (answers given), `lapses` (answers graded again), `last_result`, `last_at`, `last_correct`, `suspended`, since schema 22 `fsrs_stability` and `fsrs_difficulty` (the FSRS-6 memory state, the replay of the card's answers; NULL: not computed, see [learn-review-fsrs.md](learn-review-fsrs.md)), and since schema 19 on a sentence card `picked_level` (the level it was last picked at, `''` not yet), `picked_skipped` (the known words it met then) and `picked_at` (the hybrid clock of that pick). Times are milliseconds on the server's clock; the API sends them as ISO 8601 strings.
- One card per owner, kind and `norm` (unique): `norm` is the text trimmed, inner spaces collapsed and lower-cased, so a sentence keeps its case but is compared without it. A word is kept in lower case unless it has two or more capitals (API, OAuth); a curly apostrophe is made straight.
- `learn_reviews`: one row per answer (`card_id` with `ON DELETE CASCADE`, `owner`, `at`, `mode`, `grade`, `correct`, `answer` cut to 500 characters, and since schema 14 `uid`, a random UUID, and `sync_seq`). Schema 16, for a wrong answer only: `expected` (what was asked for, at most 300 characters; `NULL` is the card's text), `tag` (the first tag of the page's checker, `''` for none) and `hinted` (1: help was used). See "Mistakes".
- `learn_reports` (schema 17): one row per mistake report (`owner`, `uid` (primary key with the owner), `created_at`, `covers_from`, `covers_until`, `auto`, `lang` `en` | `zh`, `mistakes`, `answers`, `model`, `body` JSON `{ summary, kinds: [{ kind, count }], findings: [{ title, cause, action, cards: [{ kind, en }] }] }`, `by`, `sync_seq`). See "Mistake reports".
- Schema 14 adds to `learn_cards` `content_at`, `content_by`, `state_at`, `state_by` (the two versions of a card, below; `''` is this device) and `sync_seq` (its change number), and the table `learn_card_moves` (`owner`, `kind`, `norm`, `moved_to`, `at`, `by`, `sync_seq`; primary key owner, kind, norm). `services/learn-sync-store.js` `ensureSyncSchema` adds them to a schema 13 database before `schema.sql` runs (which makes the indexes on them): cards get content time 0, the time of their last answer (`last_at`) as state time and their id as change number; answers a random `uid` and change numbers after the cards'.
- Settings: `system_state` key `learn_v1:<owner>`, `{ collect, daily_goal, starter, audio_* }`, default `{ collect: true, daily_goal: 20, starter: 'dev' }` and the audio defaults (`AUDIO_SETTINGS` in `services/learn.js`: `audio_words` true, `audio_sentences` true, `audio_sentences_at` 'after' | 'start', `audio_repeat` 1 | 2 | 3 | 5 (2), `audio_pause` 1 | 2 | 3 seconds (2), `audio_speed` 'normal' | 'slow', `audio_stop_on_typing` true; see "Audio" in [learn-english-page.md](learn-english-page.md)), and the mistake analysis (`REPORT_SETTINGS`: `analysis_auto` true, the weekly report on; `report_seen` `''`, the report the person last opened; `report_lang` 'en' | 'zh', the language of the page that last opened Learn, which the page sets when it differs), the picking (`PICK_SETTINGS`: `pick_words` true, words are picked from the translations), the review (`SCHEDULE_SETTINGS`: `desired_retention` 0.85 | 0.9 | 0.95, default 0.9; "Desired retention" in [learn-review-fsrs.md](learn-review-fsrs.md)), with its sync version in `learn_v1_sync:<owner>` `{ at, by, seq }`. They travel with the cards in the learning sync, not in the settings sync.
- `learn_chats` (schema 20): one row per question or answer about a card (`owner`, `uid` (a random UUID, primary key with the owner), `card_id` with `ON DELETE CASCADE`, `role` `user` | `assistant`, `text` (a question at most 500 characters, an answer at most 2000), `at` (the hybrid clock), `by` (the engine that stored it, `''` this one), `sync_seq`). Only the newest 20 of a card are kept. See "Ask about a question".
- The estimated level: `system_state` key `learn_level_v1:<owner>`, `{ level, label, reason, answers, mistakes, report_at, updated_at, at, by, seq }` (times in ms; `at` / `by` / `seq` its version on the hybrid clock and its change number, like the settings). See "Your level".
- A learning card counts as user data for the empty-home check (`lib/data-home.js`), except starter words never answered.

### Review rules

FSRS-6 since schema 22: the grades, the relearning step of again, the intervals at the desired retention, the replay of a card's answers, the sync rule and the migration are in [learn-review-fsrs.md](learn-review-fsrs.md). The rules that do not depend on the scheduler, in `services/learn-review.js` (pure functions with the time passed in):

- A new card is due at once (interval 0). Starter words are the exception: they become due 10 a day (`STARTER_PER_DAY`), so the list does not fill the first review.
- **Mistake**: the last answer was wrong, or `lapses` > 0 and a wrong answer in the last 30 days.
- **Days** are calendar days in the server's time zone (`Intl.DateTimeFormat`, so a day with a clock change is one day). Streak: consecutive days with at least one answer, ending today, or yesterday while nothing was answered yet today. Done today: answers today. New today: cards created today.

### API

Behind the page login and the same-origin check like every `/api` route (the router checks both again itself). JSON; English error messages, Chinese in `public/js/i18n-zh-server.js`; a refused check is 400, a card of another owner or no card 404, a duplicate text on PATCH 409.

- `GET /summary` → `{ due, new_today, done_today, goal, streak, words, sentences, mistakes, collecting, starter, new_report }` (`new_report`: `{ uid, created_at, mistakes }` of the newest report when it is automatic and not the one in `report_seen`, else `null`). It imports the chosen starter list first if it never was.
- `GET /queue?mode=review|spell|build|dictation|say|fill|mistakes&limit=20` (limit 1–100) → `{ cards }`. review: due cards of both kinds, oldest due first; spell: word cards, due first, then the rest by least recently practised (never practised first); build, dictation, say, fill: sentence cards in the same order; mistakes: mistake cards, most recently answered first. Suspended cards never. Every queue card has `next: { again, hard, good }`, the milliseconds until it is due again if answered now with that grade (`again` is always 600000; for the labels of the grade buttons). **Practice queues load fast**: the answer waits only for the meanings and transcriptions of the first two cards (`QUEUE_HEAD`, the card shown first and the next); the words after them without a meaning or a transcription are looked up in the background after the answer (4 at a time, no more asks after the first failure; `settle()` of the service is its end, for tests), and the page asks `POST /meaning` for a card that is still without one when it is shown. Most cards already have both: they are translated ahead of time in the background (see "Meanings are translated ahead of time"), and this fill is the fallback for the cards that run has not reached yet. One lookup per word is in progress at a time (`lookingUp`): the page's `/meaning` joins the background's. While the last lookup failed (`translatorDown`, also with no translator; cleared by the next lookup that works) spell leaves out the words without a meaning. Before 2.148.1 the answer waited for every word of the 40 read, one translator call each: a list of new starter words took many seconds to open.
- `POST /cards { kind, en, zh?, phonetic?, source, context? }` → `{ card, created }`. A duplicate returns the card there, filling an empty meaning or transcription. Every card in the API has `phonetic` and `retrievability` (the FSRS probability of recall now, 0..1 to 4 decimals, from the stored stability and the real time since the last answer; `null` for a card without an FSRS state). Checks: a word 1–40 characters, letters with a hyphen or an apostrophe; a sentence 1–300 characters, at least three words (links do not count), no Chinese, not code-like (braces, two semicolons, `=>`, `&&`, `||`, a fence, `::`, `$(`, a slash command or prompt sign at the start, `name = ...`, or more than one character in seven a symbol outside sentence punctuation); `zh` at most 500. While `collect` is off, a card of source `translation` or `lookup` is not kept: `{ card: null, created: false, collecting: false }`.
- `POST /cards/:id/answer { mode, grade: 'again'|'hard'|'good', correct, answer?, expected?, tag?, hinted? }` → `{ card }`. `expected`, `tag` and `hinted` are kept for a wrong answer only (`tag` one of `MISTAKE_TAGS`, another string is kept as none; `expected` defaults to the card's text).
- `GET /mistakes?limit=50&before=<ISO>` (limit 1–200) → `{ mistakes: [{ uid, at, mode, tag, hinted, expected, written, card: { id, kind, en } }], more }`: the wrong answers, newest first. On a company computer the home's (after a sync), or the ones waiting here with `offline: true` while it cannot be reached.
- `GET /reports` → `{ reports (newest first, without findings), since: { from, mistakes, answers }, model: { ready, label, name }, running, auto }`; `GET /reports/:uid` → `{ report }`; `POST /reports { lang? }` → `{ report }` (waits for the model; 400 with no new mistakes, 409 while one runs or on a company computer without a home, 503 without a model, 502 for an answer that could not be read); `POST /cards/find { refs: [{ kind, en }] }` → `{ cards }` (the cards of a report on this engine, by text, following moves; suspended ones left out). See "Mistake reports".
- `PATCH /cards/:id { zh?, en?, suspended? }` → `{ card }` (en checked again).
- `GET /cards?kind=&source=&q=&limit=50&offset=0` (limit 1–200) → `{ cards, total }`, newest first; `q` searches the English and the meaning (`%` and `_` are plain characters); `source` one of the sources (another is 400 "Unknown card source"), and `source=translation` also gives the words asked inside a translated sentence whatever their source (the "View" list of the Learn settings asks `kind=word&source=translation`). Untouched starter words are left out while no starter list is chosen.
- `POST /meaning { word }` → `{ zh, phonetic }`: a word card's meaning if it has one, else this service's cache (1,000 words), else the translator once (`translateWord` of `services/translator.js`, through the linked service that shares one like `POST /api/translate/word`); a word card without a meaning gets it. No translator: 503 "Translation service not configured yet"; other translator errors keep their status and message.
- `POST /starter { list: 'dev' }` → `{ added }`: imports the list once (nothing when a starter word of the owner exists; words the person already has are kept) and chooses it.
- Every card in the API has `context_zh`: for a word card whose `context` is the English of one of the owner's sentence cards, that sentence's Chinese (`zh`), else `''`. It is a join when the cards are read (by the exact English, the sentence card that `context` was copied from), not a stored column: an edited meaning of the sentence shows at once and nothing more is synced; a sentence whose English is edited leaves its words with `''` (they are then picked again, below). The page asks the word inside its sentence exactly when `context_zh` is not empty and the word is found in `context`.
- `GET /level` → `{ level: { level, label, reason, answers, mistakes, report_at, updated_at } | null, picking: { enabled, picked, skipped, sentences, pending, running }, translator }` (times ISO 8601; `translator`: a chat model is set up here); `POST /level/check` → the same after a new estimate (and a pick in the background when the level changed): 400 "Answer at least 10 cards first, so your level can be estimated", 409 "Your level is being estimated already", 503 "Translation service not configured yet", 502 for an answer that could not be read. On a company computer both are asked of its home; while it is away `GET /level` is the copy here with `offline: true` and `translator: false` (`home_older: true` for a home without this version) and the check is 503 "Your level is kept on a normal computer, which cannot be reached right now". See "Your level".
- `GET /cards/:id/ask` → `{ messages: [{ uid, role, text, at }], translator }` (oldest first); `POST /cards/:id/ask { question, mode, state }` streams the answer as `application/x-ndjson`. See "Ask about a question".
- `GET /chats`, `GET /chats/:uid`, `POST /chats/send` (NDJSON), `PATCH /chats/:uid`, `DELETE /chats/:uid`: the English chats, see [learn-english-chat.md](learn-english-chat.md); `GET /summary` also has `chats`, the number of chats.
- `GET /settings`, `PUT /settings { collect?, daily_goal? (1–200), starter? ('dev' | null), audio_*?, analysis_auto?, report_seen?, report_lang?, pick_words? (boolean), desired_retention? (0.85 | 0.9 | 0.95) }` → the settings (an audio value outside its list: 400 "Invalid audio setting"; another retention: 400 "The desired retention must be 0.85, 0.9 or 0.95"; a new retention reschedules the answered cards in the same transaction). Choosing a list imports it; `starter: null` hides the starter words never answered (the answered ones stay); choosing it again shows them again.

### Mistakes

Every wrong answer keeps what was asked for (the word, the sentence, or the gap word of Fill the gap), what the person wrote, the mode, whether help was used, and a first tag from the page's checker (`mistakeTag` in `learn-grade.mjs`; the same list `MISTAKE_TAGS` in `services/learn.js`, compared by `test/learn-mistakes.test.js`): `letter-order`, `double-letter`, `missing-letter`, `extra-letter`, `wrong-letter`, `spelling`, `article`, `plural`, `tense`, `verb-form`, `word-order`, `missing-word`, `extra-word`, `wrong-word`, `listening`, `pronunciation`, `unknown` (I don't know / an empty answer), `other`. A spelled word is compared letter by letter (the same letters in another order, a doubled or undoubled letter, one letter left out, added or changed, then a spelling slip or another word); a sentence by the named mistakes of `diffWords` with a missing or extra auxiliary (has, will, is …) as tense and -ing as a verb form; Fill the gap tags the whole sentence, so another form of the gap word is grammar; a Dictation or Say it sentence with nothing named is listening or pronunciation. The detail syncs with the answer (an answer that came through an older version gets it filled in, never replaced) and follows the company computer rule like the answers.

### Mistake reports

`services/learn-analysis.js`. "Analyze" on the Mistakes page, and once a week by itself, asks the chat model the person set up for translation (`services/translator.js` `chat`: the provider, key and model in use, `response_format` JSON, 90 s) why they make their mistakes.

- **What is sent**: the wrong answers since the last report (after its `covers_until`), the newest 200 at most, each as `{ id, mode, tag, expected, written }` (texts cut to 160 characters, the whole question at most 24,000 characters), with a system prompt that asks for one JSON object `{ summary, findings: [≤ 4 { title, cause, action, items: [ids] }] }` in English or Simplified Chinese (`lang` of the request, else `report_lang`) and says the data is never instructions. Nothing else: no meaning, context, time, name or card id; only to that provider; never in tests (every test hands in a stub).
- **The report**: the answer is checked (`parseReport`: a JSON object with a summary, findings with titles; ids mapped to the cards of those mistakes, each card once, at most 30 per finding; sizes cut by `reportBody` of `learn-merge.js`); anything else is 502 "The model's answer could not be read. Try again." and nothing is stored. The kinds and their counts are made here from the tags (`kindCounts`: spelling, tense, verb-form, article, plural, word-order, words, listening, pronunciation, unknown, other), not by the model. Stored with `mistakes` (all wrong answers of the stretch), `answers`, `covers_from`, `covers_until` (the time of the run, where the next report starts) and the model's name. One run per owner at a time (409).
- **Weekly**: checked every hour (first 2–3 minutes after start). It runs when `analysis_auto` is on, no automatic report was made in the last 7 days and at least 10 wrong answers in the last 7 days are not covered by a report (`weeklyDue`, pure, with a fake clock in the tests). Only on a normal machine (never with the company computer flag), and only on the online normal engine with the smallest instance id among those whose `/v1/info` lists `learn:reports` (`weeklyRunner`; the instance ids come from each peer's `/v1/info`); it syncs once before it runs and checks again. Its id is `auto-<ISO week in UTC>`, so two engines that ran it while apart keep one after they meet (the earlier, `reportWins`). Failures are logged once per reason, without content.
- **Sync**: reports travel in the learning sync's pages (`reports`, made once, never changed; the same id keeps the earlier one), to normal machines only (`cards_only` pulls leave them out); a company computer hands any it had to its home and deletes them. The pull's `have` kind became `full-16` with this version, so every link reads the other side once from the start after the update and gets what an older version left out.
- **Company computer**: its page reads the reports (`GET /v1/learn-sync/reports`, `/report?uid=`), the wrong answers (`/mistakes`) and asks for an analysis (`POST /v1/learn-sync/analyze`) on its home, which runs it with its own model; while the home cannot be reached `/reports` says `offline` and Analyze is 503. It never asks a model itself (409).
- **Without a model**: `GET /reports` says `model.ready: false` (translation off or no key) and Analyze is 503 "Mistake analysis needs a translation model. Set one up in Settings → Voice and translation."; the list and the tags work as before. A translator on a linked service only (not set up here) is not used.
- **Known limits**: the kinds count the newest 200 mistakes of a larger stretch; an older linked version drops `analysis_auto`, `report_seen` and `report_lang` from the settings it writes back (they return to their defaults until it is updated); the week is a UTC week.

### Your level

`services/learn-pick.js`. An estimate of the person's written English on the CEFR scale (A2, B1, B2, C1), which chooses the words picked from their translations; the Learn settings show it ("MY LEVEL" in [learn-english-page.md](learn-english-page.md)).

- **What is sent**: to the chat model the person set up for translation (`chat` of `services/translator.js`, as the mistake reports; JSON, 90 s), the answered cards, most answered first (at most 200, each `{ kind, text, right, wrong, modes, mistakes: { tag: count } }`, texts cut to 160 characters, at most 24,000 characters), the totals `{ answers, right, wrong, cards }` and the summary of the newest mistake report. Never a time, a name, a written answer or a meaning. The system prompt asks for one JSON object `{ level, label, reason }` and says the record is data, never instructions.
- **The answer** is checked (`parseLevel`): a level of the list (case does not matter), an English label (cut to 40 characters) and a reason (cut to 200); anything else is 502 and nothing is stored. Stored with `answers` and `mistakes` (the totals it read), `report_at` (the report it read, or null) and `updated_at`.
- **When**: the first time picking needs a level (none yet), after each weekly mistake report (`onWeeklyReport` of `services/learn-analysis.js`, in the background), and on "Check again" (`POST /level/check`). At least 10 answers are needed (`MIN_ANSWERS`); with fewer, picking waits. One estimate at a time per owner (a second "Check again" is 409; picking joins the one going). Only on a normal engine; a company computer reads the synced copy and asks its home.
- **Sync**: the level travels in the learning sync's pages (`level: { value, at, by }`, also in a company computer's cards-only pull, as a read-only copy, like the settings) and the later version wins (a tie to the greater instance id): the newest estimate. A value that does not check is not applied.

### Picking words from translations

`services/learn-pick.js`. Words above the person's level in their own translated sentences become Spell cards asked inside the sentence ("Pick less common words, and optimize based on my English level"). On with `pick_words` (default true).

- **The question**: the sentence cards of source `translation` not picked at the current level, newest first, 10 at a time (`BATCH`), with the level, to the same chat model: "For each sentence, list the words above this learner's level that are worth learning: the form in the sentence, its Chinese meaning in the sentence", leaving out names, code, commands, file names, numbers and words at or below the level, at most 5 a sentence, as `{ sentences: [{ id, words: [{ word, zh }] }] }`. Only the sentences go out.
- **The check** (`parsePicks`): only the ids asked for; the word in the card form (lower case unless it has two or more capitals, a curly apostrophe made straight) must pass the word rule and be a whole word of its sentence (not inside a longer word: letters, an apostrophe or a hyphen on either side; case does not matter; `wholeWordIn`), with a meaning of 1–500 characters; each word once a sentence. What does not check is dropped; an answer that is not the JSON asked for is a failure.
- **What it makes**: a new word becomes a word card (`source` `translation`, `en` the form, `zh` the model's meaning, `phonetic` from the dictionary, `context` the sentence's English), due 10 a day after the picked words still waiting (`PICK_PER_DAY`, like the starter list, so a backlog does not fill one review; Spell shows them anyway). A word that has a card: **known** (`isKnown` of `services/learn-review.js`: answered right at least twice with the last answer right, or a review interval of 21 days or more) is skipped and counted; suspended is left alone; otherwise it gets the sentence as its context (a content change, so it syncs) and keeps its source, meaning (an empty one is filled) and progress, unless it is already asked inside a newer sentence (a word in two sentences keeps the newest). The sentence card gets `picked_level`, `picked_skipped` and `picked_at`. Picked words are never removed by a later pick.
- **When**: after a new translated sentence is saved (`POST /cards`, 3 s later in the background; a company computer's sentence is picked by its home after the push), when `pick_words` is turned on, after the level changed here, and every hour (first 4–5 minutes after start) for the backlog, on the online normal engine with the smallest instance id among those whose `/v1/info` lists `learn:pick` (a level that came through the sync is picked by that check). A run goes batch after batch (at most 30) and stops at the first failure of the model, logged once per reason without content; the next check tries again. Only on a normal engine, with a model set up here.
- **A new level**: every sentence not picked at it is pending again and is sent once more; words already picked stay, new ones are added. A sentence whose English is edited is pending again.
- **Counts** (`GET /level` `picking`): `picked` (word cards with source translation, plus word cards whose context is a translated sentence; not suspended), `skipped` (the known words met, the sum over the picked sentences), `sentences` (the translated sentence cards, not suspended), `pending` (those not picked at the current level; all of them while there is no level), `running` (a pick or an estimate is going).
- **Sync**: the pick of a sentence is a third part of the card with its own clock (`picked_at`): the later pick wins, a peer without it (an older version) sends none and never undoes one, and a moved card brings none. A word card's context may have up to 300 characters in the sync (other contexts 120); when an older version had cut a sentence to 120 characters, the same content version with the whole sentence restores it. The pull kind became `full-19`, so every link reads the other side once from the start after the update. `learn:pick` in `/v1/info`; the home routes `GET /v1/learn-sync/level` and `POST /v1/learn-sync/level-check` answer a company computer's page.
- **Decisions** (where the contract left room, 2026-11-21): `context_zh` is a join by the exact English, not a stored column; `GET /cards?source=translation` also lists the existing words asked inside a sentence, so "View" matches "Picked so far" (with `kind=word`); `skipped` is stored per sentence (`picked_skipped`), so every engine shows the same number; picked words are spread over days; at least 10 answers before a level; the backlog runs on one engine (`learn:pick`), the run after a new sentence on the engine that saved it; without a translator the check is 503 with the translator's message "Translation service not configured yet".
- **Known limits**: a word asked inside a sentence whose English is later edited loses `context_zh` until that sentence is picked again; two engines picking the same sentence at the same moment (both after a sentence, before they sync) ask the model twice and keep one card per word; an older linked version drops `pick_words` from the settings it writes back (on again, the default, until it is updated).

### Ask about a question

`services/learn-ask.js`. On every practice card the person can ask the model about the question in front of them ("Ask" on the card, see [learn-english-page.md](learn-english-page.md)): a hint before Check, why an answer was wrong after it, the grammar, other examples, how to remember it. No agent framework: `converse` of `services/translator.js` is the chat model the person set up for translation, made multi-turn and streamed.

- **Model** (`converse(owner, system, messages, { timeoutMs, temperature, maxTokens, onDelta, signal })`): the same provider, key, URL, model, GLM thinking switch and errors as `complete` (they share `activeProvider`, `post` and `callFailure`); `messages` are `[{ role: 'user' | 'assistant', content }]` after the system message. With `onDelta` it sends `stream: true` and reads the OpenAI-compatible server-sent events (`data: {...}` lines with `choices[0].delta.content`, `data: [DONE]`; comments, `event:` lines and a reasoning model's `reasoning_content` skipped; an `error` object in the stream is the provider's error; a provider that answers with one JSON body is one piece). It returns the whole text either way. The caller's `signal` cancels it (499 "The question was cancelled"), the timeout (60 s here) is 504, an empty answer 502 "The model gave no answer". Here: temperature 0.3, at most 800 tokens.
- **The prompt** (`systemPrompt`): a friendly English tutor for a Chinese-speaking developer; answer in the language of the question; at most about 120 words unless asked for more; English examples stay English; plain text with `**bold**` and line breaks only. The card: a word with its meaning, its transcription and, when it is asked inside one of the person's sentences (its context is the English of a sentence card), that sentence and its Chinese; a sentence with its Chinese. The practice mode in words, and the person's level (`storedLevel`) when known. **Before Check** (`state.checked` false) the model is given the right answer (the card's text, or `state.expected`: the gap word of Fill the gap) only for hints and told never to write it, spell it, list its letters, give a translation or example that gives it away, or confirm a guess; it offers hints (first letter, length, part of speech, grammar, meaning, another example) and says Check shows the answer. **After Check**: the right answer, the person's answer (or none), right or wrong and whether help was used, so it may explain freely. The card's earlier messages (at most 20, oldest first, starting with a question) go before the new question. Privacy: never a time, a name, the login, or the task a card came from (a context that is not one of the person's sentences, and a sentence card's own context, stay out).
- **Checks, in this order and before the model is asked**: the body (400 "Invalid question"), `question` 1–500 characters after trimming (400 "Enter your question", "The question cannot exceed 500 characters"; line breaks kept), `mode` one of the practice modes (400 "Unknown practice mode"), `state` `{ checked?, answer? (cut to 500), correct?, helped?, expected? (cut to 300) }` (another type: 400 "Invalid question state"; missing: not checked), the card (404 "Card not found"), a model (503 "Translation service not configured yet"), one ask per card at a time (409 "Another question about this card is being answered"), at most 30 asks a minute per owner (429 "Too many questions in a minute. Wait a moment and ask again."; a refused ask before this point does not count).
- **Stored only when the answer arrived**: the question and the answer (cut to 2000 characters) in one transaction, each with a new uid, the hybrid clock and a change number, then the card keeps its newest 20 (`pruneChats`: by `at` then `uid`, the same order everywhere); a failed or cancelled ask stores nothing. Deleting a card deletes its messages (the foreign key); a card that moves (its English edited) keeps them; a card merged into another by a move brings them along.
- **API**: `GET /api/learn/cards/:id/ask` → `{ messages: [{ uid, role, text, at }], translator }`. `POST /api/learn/cards/:id/ask { question, mode, state }`: every refusal above is the usual JSON error. The answer streams as `application/x-ndjson` (`Cache-Control: no-store`, `X-Accel-Buffering: no`): lines `{"delta":"..."}` and a last line `{"done":true,"messages":[the stored question, the stored answer]}`, or `{"error":"<message>","status":<code>}` when the model fails after its first piece (nothing stored). The response starts with the first piece, so a model that fails before it (a wrong key, its balance, a timeout) is a JSON error with its status. When the page closes the request the model's call is cancelled and nothing is stored.
- **Sync**: the messages travel in the learning sync's pages (`chats: [{ uid, kind, norm, role, text, at, by }]`, with the card they need, like an answer): a union by uid stored with the card their kind and text lead to now (following moves); a message whose card is not there is not stored (it comes again with its card), and after a merge the card keeps its newest 20 (a pruned message that comes again changes nothing). Personal like the answers: a company computer's cards-only pull has none, and messages it had from before it was marked are handed over and removed. The pull kind became `full-20`, so every link reads the other side once from the start after the update.
- **Company computer**: its page asks its home (`GET /v1/learn-sync/ask?kind=&norm=`, `POST /v1/learn-sync/ask { kind, norm, question, mode, state }`: the card by its kind and normalized text, as ids differ per engine), which checks, asks its own model and stores the messages; the answer comes as one plain JSON `{ messages }`, not streamed (the page tells the two apart by the content type). A card made there a moment ago that the home does not have yet (404 "Card not found") gets one sync round and one more try. While the home is away `GET` is `{ messages: [], translator: false, offline: true }` and `POST` 503 "Questions about a card are answered on a normal computer, which cannot be reached right now"; a home without this version: `GET` `{ messages: [], translator: false, home_older: true }` when it answers 404, `POST` 409 "The normal computer that keeps your learning data needs an update first". The company computer never asks a model itself.
- **Decisions** (where the contract left room, 2026-11-21): `learn_chats` names its card by `card_id` (with `ON DELETE CASCADE`) and the sync joins `learn_cards` for kind and norm, like the answers, instead of storing kind and norm, so moves and deletions need no extra rule; `state.expected` (optional) carries the gap word of Fill the gap, which the server cannot know; the response starts only with the first piece, so more failures come as plain JSON errors; no new capability in `/v1/info` (an older home answers 404).
- **Known limits**: the 30-a-minute limit and the one-ask-per-card rule are per engine (two engines asking at the same moment are not counted together); a home of an older version that has no `/v1/learn-sync/ask` route at all answers an HTML 404, which the company computer reads as "cannot be reached".

### Meanings are translated ahead of time

The word cards get their meaning and transcription in the background and keep them, so a queue and Spell seldom wait for the translator (before 2.150 a starter word got its meaning only when it came up, so about half of the 320 still had none).

- **What** (`services/learn.js` `prefill(owner, { max })`): the owner's word cards with no meaning (`zh = ''`) or no transcription, in practice order (shown cards first, due first, then the least recently practised, never practised first; suspended and hidden starter words last), at most `max` (40) a call. Meanings go through `meaning()` (its cache, its one lookup per word that the page's `/meaning` and the queue's fill join, and its update that keeps the content clock), 4 at a time; transcriptions come from the dictionary, else the translator once while the server runs (a meaning answer counts as that question). It stops at the first translator failure; a word whose answer could not be read is skipped while the server runs (the queue and the page still try it) and the others go on. Returns `{ tried, filled, left, failed, error }`; `missingMeanings(owner)` counts the word cards still without a meaning (for the log).
- **When** (`services/learn-prefill.js`, started in `server.js` next to the picking and stopped with it): a check 3 minutes after the start (plus up to a minute of jitter), then every hour, and a run 5 seconds after new word cards were stored (a starter list imported, a word added through `POST /cards` without a meaning or transcription, a word renamed by `PATCH`, words picked from translations; several in a row make one run; `onNewWords` of `createLearn` and `createLearnPick`, which reach the shared scheduler only once the server started it). A run takes batch after batch until nothing is left, the translator fails or 300 cards were taken (then it logs how many meanings are still missing; the next run goes on). Every timer is unref'd.
- **Where**: only with a translator set up here (`isConfigured`, the same check as the picking; it does not depend on the `pick_words` switch), never on a company computer (`cacheOnly()` in `prefill()`, the company flag in the scheduler: its cards come filled from its home). With the Learn sync on, only the engine the picking elects runs (`pickRunner(sync.me(), sync.pickPeers())`: no online normal engine that picks has a smaller instance id; none listed: this one), so linked engines do not pay twice; the filled meanings reach the others with the sync, as a filled-in meaning keeps the content clock and an empty one never wins in the merge.
- **Shared service**: the server's `/api/learn` routes and this scheduler use one learning service (`sharedLearn()` in `services/learn.js`, with `translatorLookup()`, the path of `POST /api/translate/word`), so a word both want is asked once.
- **Log**: once per failure reason, without content (`[learn-prefill] translating word cards ahead of time failed: <reason>`), once when it works again.

### Collection hooks

`public/js/learn-collect.js` (`window.LearnCollect`), loaded before `word-translate.js` and `h5.js`:

- The switch is read once per page load, at the first use (`GET /api/learn/settings`); `LearnCollect.set(on)` sets it (the Learn page calls it after `PUT /settings`). A failed read counts as off and is tried again next time. The server checks the switch again.
- Translate-and-send (`submitBox` in `h5.js`, the one path of the computer row, the phone and the columns): after the English was sent and only when the translator really translated (`translated` is not false), `LearnCollect.sentence(english, typed Chinese, task title)`. "Send as typed" never collects.
- Tap to translate (`word-translate.js`): when a meaning is shown, `LearnCollect.word(base form or the tapped word, meaning, phonetic)` (the transcription the translator gave, kept only when the dictionary has none).
- Both are fire and forget: nothing is awaited, a failure is dropped silently, a text that cannot be a card (fewer than three words, braces, longer than 300; not a plain word) is not sent, and the same text is sent once per page load.

### Phonetic transcription

A word card shows its pronunciation in IPA, American English, between slashes (/ɪkˈspaɪər/), wherever the word itself is shown (see the page). Stored on the card (`phonetic`), part of the card content.

- **Dictionary**: the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (BSD licence; `vendor/cmudict/LICENSE`), pinned to a commit of github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict in the `data` part of `scripts/vendor-libs.json` with the SHA-256 of the source and the licence. `scripts/build-phonetics.js` makes the compact file `vendor/cmudict/cmudict-<commit>.txt.br` (about 120,000 words, 538 KB; first pronunciation only, lower-case words with apostrophes and hyphens, a possessive derived instead of stored, front-coded, one character per phone; its header comment is the format) and `node scripts/fetch-vendor.js` downloads, checks, builds and checks the SHA-256 of the built text (`--check` without network). It is never in a page bundle.
- **Lookup** (`services/phonetics.js`): the file is read and parsed the first time a word is looked up (about 0.1 s), not at start, and let go after 10 minutes without a lookup. ARPAbet becomes IPA here: stress marks before the syllable (the longest legal onset), none for one syllable, unstressed AH / ER as ə / ər, a schwa between a diphthong and a final R (fire /ˈfaɪər/). Case does not matter; a hyphenated word missing as a whole is made of its parts (the later parts get a secondary stress); "<word>'s" adds -s, -z or -ɪz. A word with no entry gives `''`; a missing file logs once and gives `''`.
- **When**: a new word card takes the dictionary's transcription, else the one the page sent (tap to translate), checked (`cleanPhonetic`: slashes, one line, at most 60 characters, no markup or CJK). Cards without one are filled from the dictionary when they are read (`GET /queue`, `GET /cards`, `POST /meaning`); for a word the dictionary does not have, the queue asks the translator once while the server runs (its word answer has an American IPA `phonetic`, kept only when its dictionary form is the word itself; at most 4 at a time, stopping at the first failure); the material list never asks. A text edit to another word takes that word's transcription. A filled-in transcription keeps the content time and gets a new change number, like a filled-in meaning.
- **Sync**: `phonetic` travels with the content and merges like `zh`: an empty one never replaces a filled one (an older peer, schema 14, sends none and loses nothing here), and a card that arrives under a moved text never brings its old transcription.

### Starter list

`services/learn-starter.js`: "Programmer English" (`dev`), about 320 English words of software work (deploy, merge, refactor, latency, timeout, dependency, regression, idempotent, ...) in a rough order of how early a developer meets them. ### Sync across linked devices

The cards, the answers and the learning settings of the single user are one set on every normal machine joined by service links, a chain A–B–C included (plain remote Engines never take part). Each machine keeps its own full copy and they merge automatically; there is no home machine between normal machines. `SETTINGS_SYNC_ENABLED=false` or `LEARN_SYNC_ENABLED=false` turns it off on one service (no capability, no rounds, 404 on `/v1/learn-sync`). The owner on each side is `ensureSingleUser().login`, so the two machines may use different logins.

- **Identity.** A card is its kind and normalized text (the unique rule per owner); its integer id stays local and the page keeps using it. An answer is its `uid`. Cards are never deleted; the one way a card leaves its text is an English edit (`PATCH en` to a text with another `norm`), which writes a move `(kind, old norm) → new norm` with the edit's time. Adding the old text again later deletes the move here and makes a new card there.
- **Clocks.** One hybrid clock per database in milliseconds (`services/learn-sync-store.js`): a local change takes `max(now, newest seen + 1)`, every time read from a peer moves it forward, and it starts from the newest time stored. A card has two versions, each a time and the instance id of the device that made it: the content (`en`, `zh`, `phonetic`, `source`, `context`, `suspended`; set by an add or an edit) and the review state (`due_at`, `interval_days`, `ease`, `reps`, `lapses`, `last_result`, `last_at`, `last_correct`, and since schema 22 `fsrs_stability`, `fsrs_difficulty`; set by an answer, and recomputed from the answers after a merge, "Replay, sync and migration" in [learn-review-fsrs.md](learn-review-fsrs.md)). Starter words are imported with both times 0, so the same list imported on two machines is the same cards and any real answer or edit wins over it. A meaning filled in by `/meaning` or by adding the card again keeps the content time (an empty meaning never wins, below).
- **Merge** (`services/learn-merge.js`, pure). Each half of a card goes to the later version, a tie to the greater instance id, so an answer on one machine and a meaning edit on another both survive. A meaning or a transcription is never replaced by an empty one (so clearing a meaning does not travel), `created_at` is the earliest, and a card only one side has is copied. Answers are a union by `uid`. Settings: the later version wins; a value beats a machine that never stored one; settings from before this version get time 0; an audio value a version does not know is left out (its default there). A card that arrives under a text that moved here goes to where it moved, giving only its review state and a missing meaning (never its text); a move that arrives moves the local card into its target the same way, with its answers, unless the card was edited here after the move. A peer's card is checked like a new one (`checkEnglish`, sizes, grades, numbers) and `norm` is computed here; anything else is dropped.
- **Change numbers and pages.** Every card, answer, move and the settings version get the next number of one counter per database (`sync_seq`) whenever they are written, locally or from a peer (except on a company computer, below). Each machine keeps per link how far it has the peer's numbers (`have` in `system_state` `learn_sync_peers_v1`, with the peer's instance id; a peer whose newest number is lower than that, a restored database, is read again from 0). A round asks for the peer's rows after its `have`, one page at a time, and sends its own rows after the peer's `have` the same way, so a large first merge goes in several requests and a quiet round costs `GET /v1/info` and `GET /v1/learn-sync` only. A page holds at most 200 rows in number order (`PAGE_SIZE`, at most 1,000 asked), plus every card an answer or a move in it needs, so a receiver always has the card first. The receiver advances its `have` only when the page starts at or before it, so nothing is skipped.
- **When.** A local change (an answer, a new card, an edit, a meaning, a starter import, a settings change) calls `learnChanged`; 2 s after the last one every online normal peer gets a round. Also when a link comes online, at start and every 5 minutes. A receiver applies a row only when it changes something, and what it applied gets new numbers and a round to its other peers (the fan-out), so a chain reaches every machine and an echo stops after one page: the row sent back changes nothing.
- **Transport.** `GET /v1/info` must list `learn:sync` (and `learn:reports` for the reports, "Mistake reports") (an older peer is marked `supported: false` and skipped). Link tokens only (`linkForToken`, not revoked; any other token 403 `LINKED_ONLY`). `GET /v1/learn-sync` → `{ instance_id, public_key, company, max_seq, have }`; `POST /v1/learn-sync/pull { instance_id, public_key, after, limit, cards_only }` → `{ instance_id, sealed }`; `PUT /v1/learn-sync { instance_id, public_key, sealed }` → `{ applied, have }`. The page `{ cards, reviews, moves, settings, after, next, more }` travels only inside the settings sync's AES-256-GCM envelope, with a key derived by HKDF from that link's settings-sync key (`linkKey` of `services/settings-sync.js`, info `t-agent learn-sync v1`) and the additional data `t-agent learn-sync v1|<push|pull>|<sender>|<link id>`. Failures are logged once per link and reason by code (`[learn-sync] sync with <name> failed: <code>`).
- **Known limits.** Two machines answering the same card offline end with the replay of all the answers (FSRS, schema 22); a card whose answers are not all here yet (a page boundary, a company computer) keeps the later review state until they are. Days are calendar days of each server's time zone, so two machines in different zones can count a streak differently. A clock that runs ahead wins a little more often. Clearing a meaning does not travel. A database restored to an older copy is read again from the start by its peers, but rows its peers had sent it after the copy was taken come back only when they change again.

### Company computer

A machine can be marked as a company computer (the "Company computer" flag of "Device tier" in [linked-device-settings.md](linked-device-settings.md); K01 for this user). The person's own data does not stay there: today that is the learning data (cards, answers, settings), the usage data (button presses, "Merged across linked devices" in [usage-data.md](usage-data.md)) and the mind maps (a cache of the maps opened there, "Mind maps on every engine" in [mind-maps.md](mind-maps.md)). The shared settings and the site library still sync to it as before.

- **Home.** A company computer works through a normal machine: the online linked machine that lists `learn:sync` and whose `/v1/info` says it is not a company computer, the one with the oldest link (the order of `serviceLinks.peers()`), so the choice is stable while it stays online. The tiers come from each peer's `/v1/info`, read at every round and kept in `learn_sync_peers_v1`. The name shown while no normal machine is online is the last home (`system_state` `learn_home_v1`), else the normal machine with the oldest link.
- **Cache only.** With a normal machine known, the company computer keeps the cards (words, sentences, review state) as a cache and nothing else for long: it syncs only with its home, pulls with `cards_only` (the home leaves the answers out; the settings come as a read-only copy), applies what it pulls without new change numbers (it passes nothing on), and imports no starter list of its own. Its own changes are pushed to the home at once (0.2 s after the last one, through the same `PUT`), and after the home answers its `have`, the answers up to that number are deleted here. Normal machines never sync with a company computer themselves (their round stops after its `/v1/info`), and its `/v1/learn-sync/pull` and `PUT` answer 409 `LEARN_COMPANY_COMPUTER`. The answers and cards it had from before it was marked are handed over by the first round with the home and then removed, except the cards.
- **Reads through the home.** Before `/queue` and `/cards` the company computer syncs with its home (at most 4 s; offline, the cache as it is), so answers given elsewhere show. `/summary` is the home's (`GET /v1/learn-sync/summary`, its copy kept in `learn_home_v1`). `PUT /settings` and `POST /starter` are made on the home (`PUT /v1/learn-sync/settings`, `POST /v1/learn-sync/starter`, the home's checks and messages answering) and read back; while it cannot be reached they are 503 "Learning settings are kept on a normal computer, which cannot be reached right now". `GET /settings` reads the copy.
- **Offline.** While no normal machine is online the company computer keeps reviewing from its cache: answers and new cards are written here and wait. The summary is then the cards here (`due`, `words`, `sentences`, `new_today`) with the last copy of the home's numbers plus the answers given here since: `done_today` (the copy's when it is from today, plus the local answers), the `streak` (the copy's days plus the days of the local answers) and `mistakes` (the larger of the copy's and the local count). When a normal machine is online again the waiting changes go to it in the next round (the link coming online starts one), are removed here, and the cache is refreshed.
- **No normal machine.** When every linked machine is marked too, or no normal machine was ever seen, the company computer keeps its data as a normal machine would, and nothing moves (two company computers never sync learning data).
- **Page API.** `GET /api/learn/status` → `{ company_computer, cache_only, home_name, home_online, waiting }`; `GET /api/learn/summary` adds the same as `device` with `offline_copy_at` (the time of the copy of the home's numbers when the summary was made without the home, else `null`). `company_computer` is this machine's flag; `cache_only` is true while the learning data lives on a normal machine (only then are `home_name`, `home_online` and `waiting` set; otherwise `null`, `null`, `0`). `waiting` counts the answers here plus the cards added or edited here and the moves made here that the home has not got. The banner of the Learn page: when `cache_only` and not `home_online`, "<home_name> can't be reached. Your answers are kept on this computer for now and moved to <home_name> when it is back."
- **Known limits.** A chain normal–company–normal (T01–K01–T02 with no link between T01 and T02) does not carry the history through the company computer: the company computer works with its home only, so T02 shares learning data with T01 only through a direct link (or another normal machine). The home's summary needs the home online; offline numbers are an estimate. A page that keeps a list of cards across the moment the home goes away keeps working, because the ids are those of the cache on the company computer.
