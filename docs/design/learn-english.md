# Learn English

> Part of the T-Agent design document. The index of all parts is [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md).

## Learn English

Practice of the person's own English: the sentences they send with "Chinese → English, then send" and the words they look up with tap to translate become study cards, plus a starter word list. Progress is kept on the server, per owner, so the phone and the computer of the same person share it, and it is the same on every machine joined by service links (see "Sync across linked devices"); a machine marked as a company computer keeps only a cache (see "Company computer"). Meanings come from the translation service the person already set up; no other outside service is used.

- **Server**: `services/learn.js` (cards, queues, mistakes, summary, settings, meanings, starter import), `services/learn-review.js` (the review rules, pure), `services/learn-starter.js` (the starter lists, English only), `routes/learn.js` (`/api/learn`). Tables `learn_cards` and `learn_reviews` in `db/schema.sql` (schema 13; the sync columns and `learn_card_moves` schema 14; `learn_cards.phonetic` schema 15). The phonetic transcription: `services/phonetics.js` with the data in `vendor/cmudict/` (see "Phonetic transcription"). The sync: `services/learn-sync.js` (rounds, pages, the company computer), `services/learn-merge.js` (the merge rules, pure), `services/learn-sync-store.js` (the schema 14 columns, the hybrid clock, the change numbers, the change hook), `routes/learn-sync.js` (`/v1/learn-sync`); the flag `services/device-tier.js`, `routes/device-tier.js`.
- **Page**: `public/js/learn-collect.js` (the collection hooks, about 2 KB, a classic script combined with the others, not part of the MUI first screen); the Learn page itself is a lazy chunk of the MUI shell (`screen-learn.jsx`, with the grading in `learn-grade.mjs`).
- **Tests**: `test/phonetics.test.js` (ARPAbet to IPA, the data format on a fixture, known and unknown words, case, hyphenated words, possessives, the idle release, a missing file, the bundled file against its checksums), `test/learn-review.test.js` (the rules), `test/learn.test.js` (checks, routes, owners, meanings, starter, gates), the 12 → 13, 13 → 14 and 14 → 15 migrations and the refusal by an older app in `test/data-home.test.js`, `test/learn-sync.test.js` (the merge rules, the 13 → 14 columns, services joined by links over HTTP: both ways, a conflict, a chain, pages, an older peer, a moved card, the starter list on both sides, the company computer flag, home, cache, queue and hand-over, every machine marked), `scripts/test-h5-learn-collect-browser.cjs` (the hooks in real Chromium).

### Data

- `learn_cards`: `id`, `owner` (the page login, `req.session.user.login`), `kind` (`word` | `sentence`), `en`, `norm`, `zh` (`''` until a meaning is fetched), `phonetic` (schema 15: a word's IPA between slashes, `''` until known and always for a sentence), `source` (`translation` | `lookup` | `starter` | `manual`), `context` (where it came from, the task title, at most 120 characters), `created_at`, `due_at`, `interval_days` (real), `ease` (2.5 to start), `reps` (answers given), `lapses` (answers graded again), `last_result`, `last_at`, `last_correct`, `suspended`. Times are milliseconds on the server's clock; the API sends them as ISO 8601 strings.
- One card per owner, kind and `norm` (unique): `norm` is the text trimmed, inner spaces collapsed and lower-cased, so a sentence keeps its case but is compared without it. A word is kept in lower case unless it has two or more capitals (API, OAuth); a curly apostrophe is made straight.
- `learn_reviews`: one row per answer (`card_id` with `ON DELETE CASCADE`, `owner`, `at`, `mode`, `grade`, `correct`, `answer` cut to 500 characters, and since schema 14 `uid`, a random UUID, and `sync_seq`).
- Schema 14 adds to `learn_cards` `content_at`, `content_by`, `state_at`, `state_by` (the two versions of a card, below; `''` is this device) and `sync_seq` (its change number), and the table `learn_card_moves` (`owner`, `kind`, `norm`, `moved_to`, `at`, `by`, `sync_seq`; primary key owner, kind, norm). `services/learn-sync-store.js` `ensureSyncSchema` adds them to a schema 13 database before `schema.sql` runs (which makes the indexes on them): cards get content time 0, the time of their last answer (`last_at`) as state time and their id as change number; answers a random `uid` and change numbers after the cards'.
- Settings: `system_state` key `learn_v1:<owner>`, `{ collect, daily_goal, starter, audio_* }`, default `{ collect: true, daily_goal: 20, starter: 'dev' }` and the audio defaults (`AUDIO_SETTINGS` in `services/learn.js`: `audio_words` true, `audio_sentences` true, `audio_sentences_at` 'after' | 'start', `audio_repeat` 1 | 2 | 3 | 5 (2), `audio_pause` 1 | 2 | 3 seconds (2), `audio_speed` 'normal' | 'slow', `audio_stop_on_typing` true; see "Audio" in [learn-english-page.md](learn-english-page.md)), with its sync version in `learn_v1_sync:<owner>` `{ at, by, seq }`. They travel with the cards in the learning sync, not in the settings sync.
- A learning card counts as user data for the empty-home check (`lib/data-home.js`), except starter words never answered.

### Review rules

`services/learn-review.js`, pure functions with the time passed in:

- A new card is due at once (interval 0). Starter words are the exception: they become due 10 a day (`STARTER_PER_DAY`), so the list does not fill the first review.
- **good**: the interval times the ease, taken to the nearest step of 1, 3, 7, 15, 30 days (a tie takes the longer step) when that step is longer than the interval now; with the starting ease that is exactly 1 → 3 → 7 → 15 → 30. When the nearest step is not longer (a low ease), the product itself to 0.1 day. Past 45 days, the product in whole days. At most 365 days. The ease is unchanged.
- **hard**: the interval times 1.2, at least 1 day; ease − 0.15.
- **again**: interval 0, due in 10 minutes; ease − 0.2; lapses + 1. The ease never goes below 1.3.
- Every answer adds one to `reps` and sets `last_result`, `last_at` and `last_correct` (the `correct` of the answer; the grade alone decides the schedule).
- **Mistake**: the last answer was wrong, or `lapses` > 0 and a wrong answer in the last 30 days.
- **Days** are calendar days in the server's time zone (`Intl.DateTimeFormat`, so a day with a clock change is one day). Streak: consecutive days with at least one answer, ending today, or yesterday while nothing was answered yet today. Done today: answers today. New today: cards created today.

### API

Behind the page login and the same-origin check like every `/api` route (the router checks both again itself). JSON; English error messages, Chinese in `public/js/i18n-zh-server.js`; a refused check is 400, a card of another owner or no card 404, a duplicate text on PATCH 409.

- `GET /summary` → `{ due, new_today, done_today, goal, streak, words, sentences, mistakes, collecting, starter }`. It imports the chosen starter list first if it never was.
- `GET /queue?mode=review|spell|build|dictation|say|fill|mistakes&limit=20` (limit 1–100) → `{ cards }`. review: due cards of both kinds, oldest due first; spell: word cards, due first, then the rest by least recently practised (never practised first); build, dictation, say, fill: sentence cards in the same order; mistakes: mistake cards, most recently answered first. Suspended cards never. A word without a meaning asks for it first (4 at a time, no more asks after the first failure); spell leaves out a word whose meaning cannot be found.
- `POST /cards { kind, en, zh?, phonetic?, source, context? }` → `{ card, created }`. A duplicate returns the card there, filling an empty meaning or transcription. Every card in the API has `phonetic`. Checks: a word 1–40 characters, letters with a hyphen or an apostrophe; a sentence 1–300 characters, at least three words (links do not count), no Chinese, not code-like (braces, two semicolons, `=>`, `&&`, `||`, a fence, `::`, `$(`, a slash command or prompt sign at the start, `name = ...`, or more than one character in seven a symbol outside sentence punctuation); `zh` at most 500. While `collect` is off, a card of source `translation` or `lookup` is not kept: `{ card: null, created: false, collecting: false }`.
- `POST /cards/:id/answer { mode, grade: 'again'|'hard'|'good', correct, answer? }` → `{ card }`.
- `PATCH /cards/:id { zh?, en?, suspended? }` → `{ card }` (en checked again).
- `GET /cards?kind=&q=&limit=50&offset=0` (limit 1–200) → `{ cards, total }`, newest first; `q` searches the English and the meaning (`%` and `_` are plain characters). Untouched starter words are left out while no starter list is chosen.
- `POST /meaning { word }` → `{ zh, phonetic }`: a word card's meaning if it has one, else this service's cache (1,000 words), else the translator once (`translateWord` of `services/translator.js`, through the linked service that shares one like `POST /api/translate/word`); a word card without a meaning gets it. No translator: 503 "Translation service not configured yet"; other translator errors keep their status and message.
- `POST /starter { list: 'dev' }` → `{ added }`: imports the list once (nothing when a starter word of the owner exists; words the person already has are kept) and chooses it.
- `GET /settings`, `PUT /settings { collect?, daily_goal? (1–200), starter? ('dev' | null), audio_*? }` → the settings (an audio value outside its list: 400 "Invalid audio setting"). Choosing a list imports it; `starter: null` hides the starter words never answered (the answered ones stay); choosing it again shows them again.

### Collection hooks

`public/js/learn-collect.js` (`window.LearnCollect`), loaded before `word-translate.js` and `h5.js`:

- The switch is read once per page load, at the first use (`GET /api/learn/settings`); `LearnCollect.set(on)` sets it (the Learn page calls it after `PUT /settings`). A failed read counts as off and is tried again next time. The server checks the switch again.
- Translate-and-send (`submitBox` in `h5.js`, the one path of the computer row, the phone and the columns): after the English was sent and only when the translator really translated (`translated` is not false), `LearnCollect.sentence(english, typed Chinese, task title)`. "Send as typed" never collects.
- Tap to translate (`word-translate.js`): when a meaning is shown, `LearnCollect.word(base form or the tapped word, meaning, phonetic)` (the transcription the translator gave, kept only when the dictionary has none).
- Both are fire and forget: nothing is awaited, a failure is dropped silently, a text that cannot be a card (fewer than three words, braces, longer than 300; not a plain word) is not sent, and the same text is sent once per page load.

### Phonetic transcription

A word card shows its pronunciation in IPA, American English, between slashes (/ɪkˈspaɪər/), wherever the word itself is shown (see the page). Stored on the card (`phonetic`), part of the card content.

- **Dictionary**: the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (BSD licence; `vendor/cmudict/LICENSE`), pinned to a commit of github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict in the `data` part of `scripts/vendor-libs.json` with the SHA-256 of the source and the licence. `scripts/build-phonetics.js` makes the compact file `vendor/cmudict/cmudict-<commit>.txt.br` (about 120,000 words, 538 KB; first pronunciation only, lower-case words with apostrophes and hyphens, a possessive derived instead of stored, front-coded, one character per phone; its header comment is the format) and `node scripts/fetch-vendor.js` downloads, checks, builds and checks the SHA-256 of the built text (`--check` without network). It is never in a page bundle.
- **Lookup** (`services/phonetics.js`): the file is read and parsed the first time a word is looked up (about 0.1 s), not at start, and let go after 10 minutes without a lookup. ARPAbet becomes IPA here: stress marks before the syllable (the longest legal onset), none for one syllable, unstressed AH / ER as ə / ər, a schwa between a diphthong and a final R (fire /ˈfaɪər/). Case does not matter; a hyphenated word missing as a whole is made of its parts (the later parts get a secondary stress); "<word>'s" adds -s, -z or -ɪz. A word with no entry gives `''`; a missing file logs once and gives `''`.
- **When**: a new word card takes the dictionary's transcription, else the one the page sent (tap to translate), checked (`cleanPhonetic`: slashes, one line, at most 60 characters, no markup or CJK). Cards without one are filled from the dictionary when they are read (`GET /queue`, `GET /cards`, `POST /meaning`); for a word the dictionary does not have, the queue asks the translator once while the server runs (its word answer has an American IPA `phonetic`, kept only when its dictionary form is the word itself; at most 4 at a time, stopping at the first failure); the material list never asks. A text edit to another word takes that word's transcription. A filled-in transcription keeps the content time and gets a new change number, like a filled-in meaning.
- **Sync**: `phonetic` travels with the content and merges like `zh`: an empty one never replaces a filled one (an older peer, schema 14, sends none and loses nothing here), and a card that arrives under a moved text never brings its old transcription.

### Starter list

`services/learn-starter.js`: "Programmer English" (`dev`), about 320 English words of software work (deploy, merge, refactor, latency, timeout, dependency, regression, idempotent, ...) in a rough order of how early a developer meets them. English only (Chinese lives only in the dictionaries, `test/i18n.test.js`); meanings come through `/meaning` when a word first comes up. Another list is one more entry in `STARTER_LISTS`.

### Sync across linked devices

The cards, the answers and the learning settings of the single user are one set on every normal machine joined by service links, a chain A–B–C included (plain remote Engines never take part). Each machine keeps its own full copy and they merge automatically; there is no home machine between normal machines. `SETTINGS_SYNC_ENABLED=false` or `LEARN_SYNC_ENABLED=false` turns it off on one service (no capability, no rounds, 404 on `/v1/learn-sync`). The owner on each side is `ensureSingleUser().login`, so the two machines may use different logins.

- **Identity.** A card is its kind and normalized text (the unique rule per owner); its integer id stays local and the page keeps using it. An answer is its `uid`. Cards are never deleted; the one way a card leaves its text is an English edit (`PATCH en` to a text with another `norm`), which writes a move `(kind, old norm) → new norm` with the edit's time. Adding the old text again later deletes the move here and makes a new card there.
- **Clocks.** One hybrid clock per database in milliseconds (`services/learn-sync-store.js`): a local change takes `max(now, newest seen + 1)`, every time read from a peer moves it forward, and it starts from the newest time stored. A card has two versions, each a time and the instance id of the device that made it: the content (`en`, `zh`, `phonetic`, `source`, `context`, `suspended`; set by an add or an edit) and the review state (`due_at`, `interval_days`, `ease`, `reps`, `lapses`, `last_result`, `last_at`, `last_correct`; set by an answer). Starter words are imported with both times 0, so the same list imported on two machines is the same cards and any real answer or edit wins over it. A meaning filled in by `/meaning` or by adding the card again keeps the content time (an empty meaning never wins, below).
- **Merge** (`services/learn-merge.js`, pure). Each half of a card goes to the later version, a tie to the greater instance id, so an answer on one machine and a meaning edit on another both survive. A meaning or a transcription is never replaced by an empty one (so clearing a meaning does not travel), `created_at` is the earliest, and a card only one side has is copied. Answers are a union by `uid`. Settings: the later version wins; a value beats a machine that never stored one; settings from before this version get time 0; an audio value a version does not know is left out (its default there). A card that arrives under a text that moved here goes to where it moved, giving only its review state and a missing meaning (never its text); a move that arrives moves the local card into its target the same way, with its answers, unless the card was edited here after the move. A peer's card is checked like a new one (`checkEnglish`, sizes, grades, numbers) and `norm` is computed here; anything else is dropped.
- **Change numbers and pages.** Every card, answer, move and the settings version get the next number of one counter per database (`sync_seq`) whenever they are written, locally or from a peer (except on a company computer, below). Each machine keeps per link how far it has the peer's numbers (`have` in `system_state` `learn_sync_peers_v1`, with the peer's instance id; a peer whose newest number is lower than that, a restored database, is read again from 0). A round asks for the peer's rows after its `have`, one page at a time, and sends its own rows after the peer's `have` the same way, so a large first merge goes in several requests and a quiet round costs `GET /v1/info` and `GET /v1/learn-sync` only. A page holds at most 200 rows in number order (`PAGE_SIZE`, at most 1,000 asked), plus every card an answer or a move in it needs, so a receiver always has the card first. The receiver advances its `have` only when the page starts at or before it, so nothing is skipped.
- **When.** A local change (an answer, a new card, an edit, a meaning, a starter import, a settings change) calls `learnChanged`; 2 s after the last one every online normal peer gets a round. Also when a link comes online, at start and every 5 minutes. A receiver applies a row only when it changes something, and what it applied gets new numbers and a round to its other peers (the fan-out), so a chain reaches every machine and an echo stops after one page: the row sent back changes nothing.
- **Transport.** `GET /v1/info` must list `learn:sync` (an older peer is marked `supported: false` and skipped). Link tokens only (`linkForToken`, not revoked; any other token 403 `LINKED_ONLY`). `GET /v1/learn-sync` → `{ instance_id, public_key, company, max_seq, have }`; `POST /v1/learn-sync/pull { instance_id, public_key, after, limit, cards_only }` → `{ instance_id, sealed }`; `PUT /v1/learn-sync { instance_id, public_key, sealed }` → `{ applied, have }`. The page `{ cards, reviews, moves, settings, after, next, more }` travels only inside the settings sync's AES-256-GCM envelope, with a key derived by HKDF from that link's settings-sync key (`linkKey` of `services/settings-sync.js`, info `t-agent learn-sync v1`) and the additional data `t-agent learn-sync v1|<push|pull>|<sender>|<link id>`. Failures are logged once per link and reason by code (`[learn-sync] sync with <name> failed: <code>`).
- **Known limits.** `reps` and `lapses` are those of the later review state, so two machines answering the same card offline keep the later schedule, while both answers stay in the history. Days are calendar days of each server's time zone, so two machines in different zones can count a streak differently. A clock that runs ahead wins a little more often. Clearing a meaning does not travel. A database restored to an older copy is read again from the start by its peers, but rows its peers had sent it after the copy was taken come back only when they change again.

### Company computer

A machine can be marked as a company computer (the "Company computer" flag of "Device tier" in [linked-device-settings.md](linked-device-settings.md); K01 for this user). The person's own data does not stay there: today that is the learning data (cards, answers, settings) and the usage data (button presses, "Merged across linked devices" in [usage-data.md](usage-data.md)). The shared settings and the site library still sync to it as before.

- **Home.** A company computer works through a normal machine: the online linked machine that lists `learn:sync` and whose `/v1/info` says it is not a company computer, the one with the oldest link (the order of `serviceLinks.peers()`), so the choice is stable while it stays online. The tiers come from each peer's `/v1/info`, read at every round and kept in `learn_sync_peers_v1`. The name shown while no normal machine is online is the last home (`system_state` `learn_home_v1`), else the normal machine with the oldest link.
- **Cache only.** With a normal machine known, the company computer keeps the cards (words, sentences, review state) as a cache and nothing else for long: it syncs only with its home, pulls with `cards_only` (the home leaves the answers out; the settings come as a read-only copy), applies what it pulls without new change numbers (it passes nothing on), and imports no starter list of its own. Its own changes are pushed to the home at once (0.2 s after the last one, through the same `PUT`), and after the home answers its `have`, the answers up to that number are deleted here. Normal machines never sync with a company computer themselves (their round stops after its `/v1/info`), and its `/v1/learn-sync/pull` and `PUT` answer 409 `LEARN_COMPANY_COMPUTER`. The answers and cards it had from before it was marked are handed over by the first round with the home and then removed, except the cards.
- **Reads through the home.** Before `/queue` and `/cards` the company computer syncs with its home (at most 4 s; offline, the cache as it is), so answers given elsewhere show. `/summary` is the home's (`GET /v1/learn-sync/summary`, its copy kept in `learn_home_v1`). `PUT /settings` and `POST /starter` are made on the home (`PUT /v1/learn-sync/settings`, `POST /v1/learn-sync/starter`, the home's checks and messages answering) and read back; while it cannot be reached they are 503 "Learning settings are kept on a normal computer, which cannot be reached right now". `GET /settings` reads the copy.
- **Offline.** While no normal machine is online the company computer keeps reviewing from its cache: answers and new cards are written here and wait. The summary is then the cards here (`due`, `words`, `sentences`, `new_today`) with the last copy of the home's numbers plus the answers given here since: `done_today` (the copy's when it is from today, plus the local answers), the `streak` (the copy's days plus the days of the local answers) and `mistakes` (the larger of the copy's and the local count). When a normal machine is online again the waiting changes go to it in the next round (the link coming online starts one), are removed here, and the cache is refreshed.
- **No normal machine.** When every linked machine is marked too, or no normal machine was ever seen, the company computer keeps its data as a normal machine would, and nothing moves (two company computers never sync learning data).
- **Page API.** `GET /api/learn/status` → `{ company_computer, cache_only, home_name, home_online, waiting }`; `GET /api/learn/summary` adds the same as `device` with `offline_copy_at` (the time of the copy of the home's numbers when the summary was made without the home, else `null`). `company_computer` is this machine's flag; `cache_only` is true while the learning data lives on a normal machine (only then are `home_name`, `home_online` and `waiting` set; otherwise `null`, `null`, `0`). `waiting` counts the answers here plus the cards added or edited here and the moves made here that the home has not got. The banner of the Learn page: when `cache_only` and not `home_online`, "<home_name> can't be reached. Your answers are kept on this computer for now and moved to <home_name> when it is back."
- **Known limits.** A chain normal–company–normal (T01–K01–T02 with no link between T01 and T02) does not carry the history through the company computer: the company computer works with its home only, so T02 shares learning data with T01 only through a direct link (or another normal machine). The home's summary needs the home online; offline numbers are an estimate. A page that keeps a list of cards across the moment the home goes away keeps working, because the ids are those of the cache on the company computer.
