# The page: T-Attention calendar

> Part of the T-Agent design document. The index of all parts is [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md).

The user's request (2026-11-26): "Add a calendar view to attention, supporting schedules (recurring, one-off, birthdays (with lunar calendar support), anniversaries); the view should also display todos." Decisions: the week starts on Sunday (both languages); birthdays and anniversaries show their year number ("turns 62", "8th anniversary"); they also appear in the list view. The server side (the yearly rule, lunar dates, `occurrencesIn`, the capability `attention:yearly`) is in [attention-schedules.md](attention-schedules.md); the rest of the T-Attention page in [page-attention.md](page-attention.md).

## Calendar view

- Files: `scripts/h5-shell/AttentionCalendar.jsx` (the view and the New event dialog), `calendar-model.mjs` (pure: the grid, the events, the words), `attention-schedule.mjs` (the yearly form `yearlyRule`, `yearlyOn`, the row line `rowLine`), `Attention.jsx` (the toggle, `YearlyFields`, `lunarText`, "Every year" in the edit view). All of it is in the T-Attention chunk: `calendar-model.mjs` bundles `services/attention-schedule.js` and `lib/lunar.js` (CommonJS, no Node-only module); the first screen (`attention-model.mjs`, `theme.mjs`) only gained the colour tokens.
- **List | Calendar**: an exclusive `ToggleButtonGroup` under the panel header (`#h5-attention-view`, full width on a phone, 220px in the side sheet). The choice is `h5-attention-view` (`list` / `calendar`) of the shared page state ([shared-page-state.md](shared-page-state.md)): the same on every device and engine. In the calendar the quick add is hidden (its + makes events). On a computer the side sheet then takes the whole window right of the rail (`SIDE_SHEET_CALENDAR`), and goes back to 480px for the list.
- **Events** (`calendarEvents(items, from, to)`): every item of every engine the list shows, laid on the days of the drawn weeks: a schedule expanded with `occurrencesIn` (once, every repeat rule, yearly solar and lunar dates; the days and the wall time of the schedule's own zone, the owner's, as the edit view shows them), an item with a `due_date` and no schedule on that day as a to do. Closed items (done, failed, cancelled) are left out; an item with both a schedule and a due date shows its schedule only. Kinds (`kindOf`): `once`, `repeat`, `birthday` / `anniversary` (a yearly rule with that occasion; a yearly rule without one is a `repeat`), `todo`.
- **Colours** (theme `palette.calendar`, an MD2 theming choice recorded in AGENTS.md): once blue 200 (the primary), repeats teal 200, birthdays pink 200, anniversaries orange 300; a to do is a hollow ring in the text colour. The legend under the grid names them.
- **Month bar**: the month title (h6, "November 2026", `aria-live`), Today, previous and next month (`treeChevron`, turned 180 degrees for previous; `aria-label` = `title` "Previous month" / "Next month"; 44px on touch), and on a computer the contained "New event" button. Moving a month keeps the day of the month (the last day when shorter).
- **Grid** (`monthGrid`): Sunday first, whole weeks: 35 cells, 42 when the month reaches a sixth week (Nov 2026 starts on Sunday Nov 1; Oct 2026 on a Thursday, from Sep 27). `role="grid"` labelled by the month title, a header row of `columnheader`s (named by the full day), a `row` per week, each day a `gridcell` (`aria-selected`) holding one button: its name is the date and what is on it ("Thursday, Nov 26: Call the dentist, Water the plants, Send the invoice", or "…: nothing"), `aria-current="date"` for today. Roving focus: only the selected day is a Tab stop; the arrow keys move a day or a week, Home / End to the week's ends, Page Up / Page Down a month, and a move into another month shows it; the focus ring is the primary light outline. Days of the next and previous months are drawn in the disabled colour and select their month when tapped.
  - Phone: replaced by the large-cell month of "Views" below (`PhoneMonth`).
  - Computer: cells at least 88px tall with the number (today filled), the lunar date in the corner (`lunarText(…, { prefix: null })`: "10/18", in Chinese the month and day words) and up to two chips (the kind's tint and its lightened text; a to do outlined; "10:00 Call the dentist" for a timed once, "Mom's birthday · turns 62"), then "+N more". The selected day is tinted.
- **Day agenda** (`dayEvents`): the title ("Thursday, Nov 26", the day templates of the dictionaries) and its lunar date ("Lunar 10/18"; Chinese the words), then one row per event: the time or "All day", the kind's dot, the engine tag when several engines are listed, the title and a line (Once; the rule in words such as "Every 3 days 18:00"; "Birthday · turns 62 · Lunar 10/7"; "Anniversary · 8th anniversary"; "To do"). Order: the timed events by time, then birthdays and anniversaries, then the to-dos; equals in the list's order. A tap opens the item's edit view (the list's `EditDialog`); a to do has the round "Mark as done" button. "Nothing on this day" when empty. A computer has it in a 320px panel (the phone opens the Day view instead, "Views") at the right with **Coming up** (the next five events of the 45 days after the selected day: "Sat, Nov 28 · Pay rent" and its time, "To do", "All day" or "Lunar").
- **"N to do without a date"** (`noDateItems`: open items with neither a schedule nor a due date, in the list's order): a row at the end of the agenda (hidden at 0) that opens a dialog of the one pattern with those items; a tap opens the edit view.
- **Year numbers**: the occurrence's solar year minus `rule.year` (`yearsOn`): "turns N" for a birthday, the ordinal "Nth anniversary" (none for the year it began), nothing without a year.
- **Lunar words** (`lunarParts`, `lunarText` in `Attention.jsx`): the English is the numbers ("Lunar 10/7", "Lunar leap 6/1"); in Chinese the keys "lunar month 10", "lunar leap month 6" and "lunar day 7" of `i18n-zh-extra.js` give the traditional words (the first month, the 11th and 12th by their old names, the days 1 to 10 with their prefix, 21 to 29 with theirs), prefixed by the word for Lunar.

## Views

The user's requests (2026-11-26/27): "Add month view, week view, and day view" (Apple Calendar as the reference), "Draw the mobile month view, and also implement the year view and search", "The desktop version also needs a year view, with schedule plus duration". Mockups confirmed before building (MonthPhone, Year, YearWide, Phone day/week, WideTime, Search, Repeat).

- Files: `CalendarViews.jsx` (the phone month, the week and day time grid, the phone day's week strip, the year and its legend, `useSwipe`), `CalendarParts.jsx` (`HUE`, `KindDot`, `dayWords`, `durationWords`, `wordOr`), `calendar-views.mjs` (pure: the view key, the week, the year's months, the range and title of a view, the move by its unit, the time grid layout, the "now" line, the year's marks, durations in words), `AttentionCalendar.jsx` (the state, the switch, the bar, the computer month and the panel). All in the T-Attention chunk; the first screen did not grow (779,606 bytes before and after).
- **Year | Month | Week | Day**: an exclusive `ToggleButtonGroup` (`#h5-cal-views`): on a phone a full-width row under List | Calendar, on a computer at the start of the calendar's bar (280px). Its text is the English word, in Chinese the words of the keys "Year view" ... "Day view" (`wordOr`: "Day" and "Month" are already the date fields' words in the dictionary). 36px, 44px in touch mode. The choice is `h5-attention-cal-view` of the shared page state (`year` / `month` / `week` / `day`, month when unset; [shared-page-state.md](shared-page-state.md)): the same on every device and engine. The selected day is not shared: it is kept in a module variable while the page is loaded (closing and opening the panel keeps it; a new page starts on today).
- **One selected day drives every view** (`data-view` / `data-date` on `#h5-attention-calendar`): each view draws the days around it (`viewRange`: the year, the month grid's weeks, the Sunday-first week, the day) and expands only those (`calendarEvents`). Previous / next move it by the view's unit (`shiftView`: a year or a month keeps the day of the month, the last day when shorter; a week 7 days; a day 1), with the labels "Previous year/month/week/day" and "Next …"; Today goes to today. A switch keeps the day. Titles (`viewTitle`, templates of the dictionary): "2026", "November 2026", "Nov 22 – 28, 2026" / "Nov 29 – Dec 5, 2026" / "Dec 27, 2026 – Jan 2, 2027", "Nov 27, 2026".
- **Month on a phone** (`PhoneMonth`): large cells filling the height (5 or 6 rows, `minmax(0, 1fr)`), the number in a 26px circle (today filled, the selected day ringed), the lunar day under it (`lunarText(…, { prefix: null, short: true })`), up to two 16px chips (tint of the kind, a to do outlined; "Mom's birthday · turns 62"), the second "+N more" when there are three or more. A tap opens that day in the Day view (the grid fills the screen, so there is no agenda under it); the keys and roving focus are those of the computer month. "N to do without a date" is a row under the grid (left, clear of the +). The legend is left out on a phone. **Month on a computer**: as before; a double click on a day, or the panel's day title (`#h5-cal-day-open`), opens the Day view.
- **Week and day** (`TimeGrid`): the heads (week: the weekday letter / short name and the number, a tap opens that day; computer: the lunar day beside it), the all-day row (`allDayEvents`: to-dos with a due date, birthdays, anniversaries and schedules with `all_day`; up to 112px, then it scrolls) and 24 hours (48px an hour, 40px in the phone week; the hour labels in 24-hour time). A timed event is a block (`timedLayout`): from its start for its duration, one hour without one (`DEFAULT_MINUTES`), at least 30 minutes drawn (`MIN_SHOWN`), never past midnight; events whose blocks overlap form a cluster and each takes the first free column, all 1/n wide (side by side). The block has the kind's tint, the title and, when tall enough and not the narrow phone week, "10:00–11:30" (`timeRange`; only the start without a duration). The red "now" line (the theme's error colour, with a dot) is in today's column, moved every minute (the device's clock; the times are the owner's wall times). On opening, and when the days change, the hours scroll to an hour before now on today (16:00 at most) and to 8:00 on other days (`scrollHour`). A tap on a block or an all-day chip opens the edit view.
  - Phone day: the week strip (`WeekStrip`, a tap selects a day and stays in the day view), "Thursday, Nov 26, 2026" and the lunar date, the all-day row, the hours. Phone week: 7 narrow columns. A swipe left / right with one finger moves a day or a week (`useSwipe`: at least 50px, mostly sideways, under 800 ms; touch events only, nothing prevented, so vertical scrolling is unchanged); the phone month moves by a month the same way.
  - Computer week: the whole width (no panel). Computer day: one wide column and the 320px panel with the day's title and lunar date, "All day" (only those items) and Coming up.
- **Year** (`YearView`): phone: 3 x 4 mini months, each one button (the name, primary for the current month; the days with today filled and a dot under a day with something on it, pink for a birthday or an anniversary: `yearMarks`); a tap opens that month in the Month view (the day of the month kept); the arrow keys move a month or a row, Page Up / Down a year. Computer: 4 x 3 months, each a `grid` of day buttons (30 x 26) with the same dots; a click selects a day (the panel shows its agenda and Coming up), a double click or the panel's title opens it in the Day view, the month's name opens the Month view, and the keys of the month grid move across months (into another year too). The legend "A day with something on it" / "Birthday or anniversary" is under the year.
- Touch targets: every button is 44px in touch mode except the event blocks and all-day chips of the time grid, which are drawn to their time like the month chips (a 30-minute block is 24px tall); the day, its agenda (computer) and the edit view are reachable from larger targets.
- No animation is added (reduced motion needs nothing more); the views are `role` grid / list / group with labels as above.

## Search

- `AttentionSearch.jsx` and `calendar-search.mjs` (pure). The magnifier in the panel's header (`#h5-attention-search`, the library's `search` icon) in the list and in the calendar opens it: full screen on a phone (back arrow), a dialog on a computer (close button, at most 720px high); Escape closes it.
- A field (autofocus, clear button), filter chips (MUI `Chip`, outlined, the chosen one tinted; 44px in touch mode): All, Schedules (once and repeating), Birthdays, Anniversaries, To do (a due date or no date at all), and Include finished (a toggle).
- Matching (`searchItems`): every item the list has, of all linked engines; the title, the notes, the result and the custom status, and for a lunar yearly rule "lunar 10/7" / "10/7" (`lunarSearchText`) and, from the page, the Chinese words of that date (`extra`). NFKC and lower case (full-width letters and digits match), every word of the query must be found. Groups (`SEARCH_GROUPS`): Next up (the next day of each open item, soonest first; a lunar leap month is looked for up to 100 years ahead), Earlier this year and Earlier (an item whose days are past: an old once or an overdue to do), No date, Finished (only with Include finished). Each item once.
- A row: the date block (the month in capitals, the day; Chinese the month word), the kind's dot, the engine tag with several engines, the title with the matched text marked (`highlightParts`, a `mark` in the primary tint) and "turns 63" / "9th anniversary" (the number on that day), then "Thu, Nov 4, 2027 · Lunar 10/7 · Every year" (the year only when it is not this year; the rule in words, Once, To do or No date). A tap closes the search and opens the item's edit view. The count ("1 result", "N results", "No results") or, before typing, what is searched.

## Duration and all day

- The engine side is in [attention-schedules.md](attention-schedules.md) ("Duration and all day"): `schedule.duration` (1..1439 minutes, ending by midnight) or `schedule.all_day`, capability `attention:duration`.
- `calendarEvents` gives each event `duration` (or null) and `allDay` (to-dos, birthdays, anniversaries, `all_day` schedules; then `time` is null).
- The form (`ScheduleFields`, for Once and for a repeat that is not a birthday or an anniversary: `durationApplies`; shown when the item's engine lists the capability, `durationOn`: this machine always, or when the item already has a length): a row of "Duration" (native select: Not set, 15 min, 30 min, 45 min, 1 hour, 1.5 hours, 2 hours, 3 hours, Custom… with a Minutes field) and "Ends at" (a time). Each follows the other (`formEnd`, `durationFromEnd`); a new start keeps the duration and moves the end. An end that is not after the start would be after midnight and is refused with the line "It would end after midnight: choose an earlier end or a shorter duration" (00:00 is midnight itself, the latest end); a start and duration that pass midnight say the same, and Save refuses both. "All day" is a checkbox (the form is saved with a button) that hides the time, the duration and the end; the reminder keeps its time.
- The edit view echoes a saved length (`scheduleForm`: `duration`, `durationCustom`, `allDay`); one without a length shows "Not set" and saves none. `schedulePatch` compares the length by meaning. New event starts at 1 hour; on an engine without the capability the row is hidden and nothing about it is sent. The preview of the form asks the engine for the times only (no length), so an older engine does not refuse it.
- The list's row line (`rowLine`): "Once · tomorrow 10:30–11:30", "Weekdays 10:30–12:00 · next …", "Every day all day · …", "Once · tomorrow all day". The agenda: the end under the start time and "Once · 1 hour" (`durationWords`).

## New event

- The dialog (`#h5-cal-new`, the one dialog pattern: h6 "New event" with a close button, Cancel and a contained Save; full screen on a phone, a long form; "sm" on a computer). Opened by the phone's floating + (`Fab`, primary, 56px, bottom right of the calendar) or the computer's "New event". Starts on the selected day.
- Chips (an exclusive `ToggleButtonGroup`, 44px tall, wrapping): One-off / Repeats / Birthday / Anniversary, the selected one tinted with its kind's colour. "on <engine>" (the quick add's `h5-attention-add-on` choice, shared) when more than one engine takes new items.
- One-off and Repeats: Title, then the When and Then fields of the edit view (`ScheduleFields` with `fixed`: the chips replace its When toggle; the owner's preview line). Save: `POST <engine> { title, schedule, then_action? }` (`then_action` only for a prompt or a script).
- Birthday and Anniversary: Name, then `YearlyFields`: Calendar Solar | Lunar, Month and Day (native selects; a lunar month and day by their Chinese words in Chinese, else by number), "Birth year" / "Year it began" (optional, 1900–2100) and, for a lunar date, "Leap month" (a checkbox with "Only when the date is in a leap month": it is part of a form saved with a button, so not a switch). The card "Next birthday" / "Next anniversary" (`nextYearly`: up to 100 years ahead) shows the next solar date with its year and number ("Thu, Dec 10, 2026 · turns 66") and, for a lunar date, "Lunar 11/2. The solar date changes every year."; a date that does not come says so. Save: `{ title: "<Name>'s birthday" | "<Name> anniversary" (a name that already ends with the word stays), schedule: { kind: 'repeat', rule: { freq: 'yearly', calendar, month, day, leap?, occasion, year?, time: '09:00' } } }`. The form's errors: "Write a name", "This month has no such day" (Feb 30), "The lunar day is 1 to 30", "The year is 1900 to 2100".
- **Capability gate** (`yearlyOn(state.sources, engine)`): this machine always; another engine when its `/info` lists `attention:yearly`. Without it the Birthday and Anniversary chips are left out and the line "Birthdays and anniversaries: This computer needs an update first" is shown; "Every year" is not offered in the Repeats select either.
- No "remind me before" in this version (the server has no lead time).

## The edit view and the list

- The edit view's Repeats select has "Every year" (on an engine with the capability, or for an item that already has a yearly rule): "What it is" (A yearly reminder / Birthday / Anniversary) and `YearlyFields`. `scheduleForm` reads `calendar`, `month`, `yearDay`, `leap`, `occasion`, `year`; `scheduleFromForm` builds the rule with `yearlyRule` (the server's canonical key order); `schedulePatch` compares yearly rules by meaning (calendar, month, day, leap, occasion, year, time).
- A yearly item is a repeat item: the list's Recurring section, with the row line of `rowLine` (attention-schedule.mjs, so the first screen does not grow): "Every year · 11-04 · lunar 10/7 · turns 63" (its next day from `next_at`, in this device's MM-DD like the other row lines; the lunar date of a lunar rule; the number of the NEXT occurrence), "Every year · 11-12 · 9th anniversary"; while due the day it came ("· due now"); then done and the action as for every rule.

- Limits: the "now" line uses this device's clock while the blocks use the owner's wall times (an engine in another zone is off by the difference); an occurrence is drawn on its start day only (a timed one never passes midnight, an all-day one is one day); the phone has no agenda list (the Day view replaces it).
