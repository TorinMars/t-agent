# The page: T-Attention calendar

> Part of the T-Agent design document. The index of all parts is [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md).

The user's request (2026-11-26): "Add a calendar view to attention, supporting schedules (recurring, one-off, birthdays (with lunar calendar support), anniversaries); the view should also display todos." Decisions: the week starts on Sunday (both languages); birthdays and anniversaries show their year number ("turns 62", "8th anniversary"); they also appear in the list view. The server side (the yearly rule, lunar dates, `occurrencesIn`, the capability `attention:yearly`) is in [attention-schedules.md](attention-schedules.md); the rest of the T-Attention page in [page-attention.md](page-attention.md).

## Calendar view

- Files: `scripts/h5-shell/AttentionCalendar.jsx` (the view and the New event dialog), `calendar-model.mjs` (pure: the grid, the events, the words), `attention-schedule.mjs` (the yearly form `yearlyRule`, `yearlyOn`, the row line `rowLine`), `Attention.jsx` (the toggle, `YearlyFields`, `lunarText`, "Every year" in the edit view). All of it is in the T-Attention chunk: `calendar-model.mjs` bundles `services/attention-schedule.js` and `lib/lunar.js` (CommonJS, no Node-only module); the first screen (`attention-model.mjs`, `theme.mjs`) only gained the colour tokens.
- **List | Calendar**: an exclusive `ToggleButtonGroup` under the panel header (`#h5-attention-view`, full width on a phone, 220px in the side sheet). The choice is `h5-attention-view` (`list` / `calendar`) of the shared page state ([shared-page-state.md](shared-page-state.md)): the same on every device and engine. In the calendar the quick add is hidden (its + makes events). On a computer the side sheet then takes the whole window right of the rail (`SIDE_SHEET_CALENDAR`), and goes back to 480px for the list.
- **Events** (`calendarEvents(items, from, to)`): every item of every engine the list shows, laid on the days of the drawn weeks: a schedule expanded with `occurrencesIn` (once, every repeat rule, yearly solar and lunar dates; the days and the wall time of the schedule's own zone, the owner's, as the edit view shows them), an item with a `due_date` and no schedule on that day as a to do. Closed items (done, failed, cancelled) are left out; an item with both a schedule and a due date shows its schedule only. Kinds (`kindOf`): `once`, `repeat`, `birthday` / `anniversary` (a yearly rule with that occasion; a yearly rule without one is a `repeat`), `todo`.
- **Colours** (theme `palette.calendar`, an MD2 theming choice recorded in AGENTS.md): once blue 200 (the primary), repeats teal 200, birthdays pink 200, anniversaries orange 300; a to do is a hollow ring in the text colour. The legend under the grid names them.
- **Month bar**: the month title (h6, "November 2026", `aria-live`), Today, previous and next month (`treeChevron`, turned 180 degrees for previous; `aria-label` = `title` "Previous month" / "Next month"; 44px on touch), and on a computer the contained "New event" button. Moving a month keeps the day of the month (the last day when shorter).
- **Grid** (`monthGrid`): Sunday first, whole weeks: 35 cells, 42 when the month reaches a sixth week (Nov 2026 starts on Sunday Nov 1; Oct 2026 on a Thursday, from Sep 27). `role="grid"` labelled by the month title, a header row of `columnheader`s (named by the full day), a `row` per week, each day a `gridcell` (`aria-selected`) holding one button: its name is the date and what is on it ("Thursday, Nov 26: Call the dentist, Water the plants, Send the invoice", or "…: nothing"), `aria-current="date"` for today. Roving focus: only the selected day is a Tab stop; the arrow keys move a day or a week, Home / End to the week's ends, Page Up / Page Down a month, and a move into another month shows it; the focus ring is the primary light outline. Days of the next and previous months are drawn in the disabled colour and select their month when tapped.
  - Phone: the number in a 30px circle (today filled with the primary colour, the selected day ringed) and up to three dots, one per kind; each day button is 48px tall and at least 44px wide down to 320px.
  - Computer: cells at least 88px tall with the number (today filled), the lunar date in the corner (`lunarText(…, { prefix: null })`: "10/18", in Chinese the month and day words) and up to two chips (the kind's tint and its lightened text; a to do outlined; "10:00 Call the dentist" for a timed once, "Mom's birthday · turns 62"), then "+N more". The selected day is tinted.
- **Day agenda** (`dayEvents`): the title ("Thursday, Nov 26", the day templates of the dictionaries) and its lunar date ("Lunar 10/18"; Chinese the words), then one row per event: the time or "All day", the kind's dot, the engine tag when several engines are listed, the title and a line (Once; the rule in words such as "Every 3 days 18:00"; "Birthday · turns 62 · Lunar 10/7"; "Anniversary · 8th anniversary"; "To do"). Order: the timed events by time, then birthdays and anniversaries, then the to-dos; equals in the list's order. A tap opens the item's edit view (the list's `EditDialog`); a to do has the round "Mark as done" button. "Nothing on this day" when empty. A phone has the agenda under the grid and the legend; a computer has it in a 320px panel at the right with **Coming up** (the next five events of the 45 days after the selected day: "Sat, Nov 28 · Pay rent" and its time, "To do", "All day" or "Lunar").
- **"N to do without a date"** (`noDateItems`: open items with neither a schedule nor a due date, in the list's order): a row at the end of the agenda (hidden at 0) that opens a dialog of the one pattern with those items; a tap opens the edit view.
- **Year numbers**: the occurrence's solar year minus `rule.year` (`yearsOn`): "turns N" for a birthday, the ordinal "Nth anniversary" (none for the year it began), nothing without a year.
- **Lunar words** (`lunarParts`, `lunarText` in `Attention.jsx`): the English is the numbers ("Lunar 10/7", "Lunar leap 6/1"); in Chinese the keys "lunar month 10", "lunar leap month 6" and "lunar day 7" of `i18n-zh-extra.js` give the traditional words (the first month, the 11th and 12th by their old names, the days 1 to 10 with their prefix, 21 to 29 with theirs), prefixed by the word for Lunar.

## New event

- The dialog (`#h5-cal-new`, the one dialog pattern: h6 "New event" with a close button, Cancel and a contained Save; full screen on a phone, a long form; "sm" on a computer). Opened by the phone's floating + (`Fab`, primary, 56px, bottom right of the calendar) or the computer's "New event". Starts on the selected day.
- Chips (an exclusive `ToggleButtonGroup`, 44px tall, wrapping): One-off / Repeats / Birthday / Anniversary, the selected one tinted with its kind's colour. "on <engine>" (the quick add's `h5-attention-add-on` choice, shared) when more than one engine takes new items.
- One-off and Repeats: Title, then the When and Then fields of the edit view (`ScheduleFields` with `fixed`: the chips replace its When toggle; the owner's preview line). Save: `POST <engine> { title, schedule, then_action? }` (`then_action` only for a prompt or a script).
- Birthday and Anniversary: Name, then `YearlyFields`: Calendar Solar | Lunar, Month and Day (native selects; a lunar month and day by their Chinese words in Chinese, else by number), "Birth year" / "Year it began" (optional, 1900–2100) and, for a lunar date, "Leap month" (a checkbox with "Only when the date is in a leap month": it is part of a form saved with a button, so not a switch). The card "Next birthday" / "Next anniversary" (`nextYearly`: up to 100 years ahead) shows the next solar date with its year and number ("Thu, Dec 10, 2026 · turns 66") and, for a lunar date, "Lunar 11/2. The solar date changes every year."; a date that does not come says so. Save: `{ title: "<Name>'s birthday" | "<Name> anniversary" (a name that already ends with the word stays), schedule: { kind: 'repeat', rule: { freq: 'yearly', calendar, month, day, leap?, occasion, year?, time: '09:00' } } }`. The form's errors: "Write a name", "This month has no such day" (Feb 30), "The lunar day is 1 to 30", "The year is 1900 to 2100".
- **Capability gate** (`yearlyOn(state.sources, engine)`): this machine always; another engine when its `/info` lists `attention:yearly`. Without it the Birthday and Anniversary chips are left out and the line "Birthdays and anniversaries: This computer needs an update first" is shown; "Every year" is not offered in the Repeats select either.
- No "remind me before" in this version (the server has no lead time).

## The edit view and the list

- The edit view's Repeats select has "Every year" (on an engine with the capability, or for an item that already has a yearly rule): "What it is" (A yearly reminder / Birthday / Anniversary) and `YearlyFields`. `scheduleForm` reads `calendar`, `month`, `yearDay`, `leap`, `occasion`, `year`; `scheduleFromForm` builds the rule with `yearlyRule` (the server's canonical key order); `schedulePatch` compares yearly rules by meaning (calendar, month, day, leap, occasion, year, time).
- A yearly item is a repeat item: the list's Recurring section, with the row line of `rowLine` (attention-schedule.mjs, so the first screen does not grow): "Every year · 11-04 · lunar 10/7 · turns 63" (its next day from `next_at`, in this device's MM-DD like the other row lines; the lunar date of a lunar rule; the number of the NEXT occurrence), "Every year · 11-12 · 9th anniversary"; while due the day it came ("· due now"); then done and the action as for every rule.

## Tests and limits

- `test/attention-calendar-model.test.js`: the Sunday-first grid (Nov 2026, Oct 2026, a six-week month), events of each rule, closed items, the agenda order, Coming up, lunar and leap-month years, Feb 29, ends, the words and ordinals, year numbers, the No date list, the yearly form and its patch, the capability, the yearly row line. `test/attention-schedule-model.test.js` checks the new form fields.
- `scripts/test-h5-attention-calendar-browser.cjs` (Playwright, fixed clock Thursday 2026-11-26 10:00 in Asia/Taipei, the mocked API answering with the engine's own rules): phone 390 touch (toggle and its shared value, grid, dots, today, agenda, month navigation, No date dialog, edit of a yearly item, a lunar birthday and an anniversary created and shown in the calendar and the Recurring list, 44px targets, no overflow), computer 1440 mouse (the wide sheet, chips and "+1 more", lunar corner, 320px panel, Coming up, keyboard, a one-off, the capability gate), Chinese (titles, weekday letters, lunar words, the row line). `H5_SHOT_DIR=<dir>` saves `cal-*.png`.
- Limits: the days of an event are those of its owner's zone (a page in another zone shows the owner's date); "ends after N" counts from the start, so a schedule that has already fired may show a few days more than it will fire (`occurrencesIn`); the calendar shows only what the list holds (at most 200 tasks).
