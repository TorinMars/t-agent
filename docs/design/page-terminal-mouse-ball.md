# The page: mouse ball of the phone terminal

> Part of the T-Agent design document. The index of all parts is [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md).

## Mouse ball

Why (the user, 2026-11-26): copying from the phone terminal felt unusable; the double / triple tap selects only a word or a line and the copy view is a separate screen. The mouse ball is a floating ball that works like a mouse, after the "fv floating ball" app. Mockup confirmed by the user (seven boards: idle, drag, press, select, copied, menu, reaching the far right).

- **Where and when.** Phone only: touch input mode (`state.input === 'touch'`), the narrow layout (`!state.wide`), not in a column (`!state.pane`). OFF by default. The switch is "Mouse ball" in the session header's More menu (`#h5-mouse-ball-switch`, `role="menuitemcheckbox"` with `aria-checked`, the self-drawn `mouseBall` pointer icon, an MD2 switch drawn by one span because MUI's Switch is not in the first screen), right after "Terminal width" and shown only while a terminal is shown (the same condition as Terminal width, see "Terminal width (Fit / Wide)" in [page-terminal-and-bottom-bar.md](page-terminal-and-bottom-bar.md)). A tap turns it on or off and closes the menu. The choice is per device (localStorage `h5-mouse-ball` = `on`, else nothing; category hardware: a touch screen without a mouse wants it, one with a trackpad or a pen may not; `SHARED_KEYS` in `h5.js`, so not per pane). `h5.js` publishes it as `state.mouseBall` (`ui.actions.setMouseBall`).
- **Code.** `scripts/h5-shell/MouseBall.jsx` (drawing and touch handling), `scripts/h5-shell/mouse-ball-model.mjs` (pure geometry and the dwell reducer), the chunk `screen-mouse-ball.jsx` registered in `lazy-screens.jsx` as `MouseBallScreen` (open while on in that mode; drawn by `App.jsx` with `BALL_KEYS`), so the first screen carries only the menu row, the store key and the icon (about 1.3 KB) and the chunk (about 9 KB) is loaded only while the ball is on. `h5.js` `ui.actions.mouseBallTarget()` hands the ball the shown terminal at each touch: `{ term, el, screen, copy, hookSelection }`, or null while there is none (empty, a hidden or disposed session, the agent chat over it, the copy view open). The ball also hides while the terminal area is empty, a notebook, the mind maps or the Learn page are shown, and it looks again every 500 ms and on resize, `visualViewport` resize and scroll and a resize of `#h5-terminal`.
- **Idle.** A 52px round ball (blue grey 700, a 1px light edge, elevation 6, opacity 0.55, the `mouseBall` icon at 22px), `position: fixed` above the terminal (z-index `speedDial`, under menus, dialogs and the notice), docked at a FIXED place: 2px in from the right edge of the visible terminal, its centre two thirds down. The visible terminal is `#h5-terminal` within the visual viewport, so it stays above the on-screen keyboard and never covers the key rows (`#h5-bottom` is below `#h5-terminal`). It takes touches only on itself; the terminal keeps its scrolling, taps and long press. Over the wheel strip it covers 52px of the strip's height.
- **Drag.** Touch the ball and move: it follows the finger from where it was grabbed (`ballFor`), its centre stays inside the visible terminal. The cursor is the top-left corner of a square: two lines of 19px (2.5px white over a 5px black outline, butt caps, a mitred corner) meeting at the corner, which is the mouse position. It is drawn 46px left of and 46px above the ball's centre, with a thin dashed line (white 45%, 1.5px, 3 4) between them, in one full-viewport SVG (`pointer-events: none`, `aria-hidden`).
- **Edge rule** (user-approved, "Reaching the far right"): the finger cannot pass the screen edge, so a cursor 46px to the left would stop 46px short of the last column. The sideways offset shrinks linearly to 0 over the last 80px before the right edge of the visible terminal and the upward offset over the last 80px above its bottom edge (`cursorOffset`); at the edge the cursor is straight above (or at) the ball and reaches the last column and the last row, in between it glides without a jump. The grab offset fades the same way, relative to how much of the fade there was at the touch (`ballFor`), so a ball grabbed on its right side still reaches the edge and a touch on the docked ball (which is inside the fade zone) does not jump. The cursor is kept inside the visible cells (`cursorFor`, the screen's box within the visible terminal).
- **Dwell = button down.** With the finger down, a ball that moved less than 6px for 600 ms presses the left button at the cursor (`dwellStep`, a pure reducer over `down` / `move` / `tick` / `up` / `cancel` with the event times; a move of more than 6px restarts the wait from there; a move that comes after the wait was over presses first, where the ball stood). While waiting, a ring (an SVG circle just outside the ball, the primary colour, 3px) fills over the 600 ms; with `prefers-reduced-motion` it does not fill and shows whole once pressed. On the press: a haptic tick (Osk.jsx `keyTick`, the strength of Settings > Keyboard for this device, nothing when it is Off), the ball turns `notice` blue, the cursor turns the primary blue (`#90caf9`), `aria-pressed` is true.
- **Two modes** (2026-11-27, the user: "Change the floating ball selection"). Who gets the button is decided once, at the press (`buttonMode` in `mouse-ball-model.mjs`, from xterm's `term.modes.mouseTrackingMode`), and kept until the lift or cancel, even when the program changes its mouse mode in the middle of the gesture (the events it no longer asks for are then dropped by xterm):
  - **page** (`none`: a plain shell, Claude Code without its fullscreen mode, Codex): the page selects and copies (Select, Scrolling while selecting, Lift and Copied text below).
  - **program** (`x10`, `vt200`, `drag`, `any`: Claude Code in fullscreen, vim, tmux, opencode): the ball is the program's mouse. Programs such as these select by themselves (Claude Code knows where it wrapped its lines, a double click selects a word or an address, a triple click a line) and copy on the release through OSC 52 or pbcopy, which arrive through `terminal-clipboard.js` like any program copy. The ball dispatches real (untrusted) `mousedown` / `mousemove` / `mouseup` events with `button` 0 and `buttons` 1 while held at the cursor, and `WheelEvent`s (`deltaMode` line, one per line) for the edge scroll, on xterm's `.xterm-screen`; xterm turns them into the reports of the program's tracking mode and encoding (X10, SGR 1006, ...) for the cell under the cursor (1-based screen cells, not buffer rows) and sends them through `term.onData` like a real mouse (`send` in `h5.js` does not move the wide view for a mouse report). What is sent follows the mode (`programReports`): the press always; the release not in x10; the motion with the button held in drag and any (xterm reports one per changed cell); wheel steps not in x10 (in x10 xterm would turn a wheel event into arrow keys or its own scroll). No pointer, touch or click events are made, so the page's tap chain, long press, copy view and copy-on-select do not run, and `terminal-clipboard.js` counts only trusted events anyway. xterm focuses its textarea on the `mousedown` as for any click (it has `inputmode="none"`, so no system keyboard). An old page highlight is cleared at the press; no page highlight is drawn (the program draws its own).
  - How a person tells them apart: the ball, the ring and the blue cursor look the same in both; in program mode the highlight is the program's own (Claude Code's colours, not xterm's) and nothing is copied before the program says so; the ball carries `data-mode` (`page` / `program`) while pressed. Claude Code turns mouse reporting on only in its fullscreen mode (`/tui fullscreen` or `CLAUDE_CODE_NO_FLICKER=1`, the default for new users); without it the ball selects in the page.
- **Select** (page mode). With the button down the text from the press point to the cursor is selected with xterm's own selection (`term.select(col, row, length)`; xterm draws the highlight in the DOM renderer). Both ends are cells of the buffer (`cellFromPoint`: the column boundary nearest to the point, as a mouse selection counts it, and the screen row plus `buffer.active.viewportY`), so one start and one length cover several rows, wrapped or not (`selectionRange`); `getSelection` joins wrapped rows and ends real lines with a newline. The xterm screen's bounding box includes the 4px inset, the sideways offset of Wide mode and the keyboard shift. New output clears the highlight as after a double tap (`hookSelection`); the ball draws it again at once while the button is down.
- **Scrolling while selecting.** With the button down and the cursor within 24px of the top or bottom edge of the visible cells, the terminal scrolls by lines (`term.scrollLines`, every frame, counted by real time): 4 lines per second at the inner edge of the zone up to 40 at the edge (`scrollSpeed`); it stops when the cursor leaves the zone or the finger is lifted. The press point stays on its buffer cell, so the selection grows with the scroll. In the alternate buffer there is nothing to scroll. In program mode the same zone and speed send wheel steps up (SGR button 64) or down (65) at the cursor instead, so the program scrolls its own view (and extends its selection, if it does that); the page view does not move. Speed and the carried fraction: `scrollStep`.
- **Lift.** In program mode the lift first marks a gesture for the terminal's clipboard (`mouseBallTarget().gesture`, `handle.gesture()` of `terminal-clipboard.js`) and then sends the release at the cursor; the ball copies nothing itself. The program's copy that follows within `GESTURE_WINDOW_MS` (1500 ms) is written at once with the notice "Copied N characters" instead of the "Copy request from the terminal" bar; when the browser refuses that write (the program's copy arrives after the user activation of the lift, as on iOS Safari) the refusal bar finishes it. In page mode, if the button was down, it goes up and the selection is copied through the copy path of the double tap (`terminal-clipboard.js` `handle.copy(text)`, called inside the `pointerup`, so the browser still counts the user activation): the notice "Copied N characters", the refusal bar `#h5-clipboard-bar` ("Press Copy to finish copying") when the browser says no, the duplicate merge. The highlight stays until the next tap or new output. The ball docks back to its fixed place (a 220 ms transition, none with reduced motion). Without a press, lifting only hides the cursor and docks the ball: a plain drag positions, nothing is clicked. A cancelled touch (`pointercancel`, `lostpointercapture`) ends everything: nothing is copied and the selection is cleared; in program mode the program still gets the release (it must not be left with its button down, and xterm drops its document listeners on it), without a gesture mark, so a copy it makes then waits on the request bar. The same release goes out when the terminal goes away during the touch.
- **Copied text** (page mode; v2.174.1: "copying a wrapped line turns into a newline"). The text is not `term.getSelection()` but the rows of the selection read from the buffer and joined by `joinSelection` in `mouse-ball-model.mjs`. A row xterm soft-wrapped continues the one before it as it is. Claude Code and Codex cut their own lines at the terminal width with real line breaks, which xterm cannot know: a row after a row that runs to the right edge (within `HARD_WRAP_SLACK` = 2 columns, a long word or path cut in the middle: joined with nothing, or with one space when the row has words) or after a row whose next word would not have fitted (a break at a space: joined with one space) is the rest of the same line. A row that starts a list item, a quote, a heading or a table row, a blank row, and a row after a short row stay new lines; a selection that stops inside a row does not join it. The same rule is not applied to the double and triple tap copies.
- **The terminal underneath.** While the ball is used the terminal neither scrolls, pans, long-presses (the copy view) nor double-tap-selects: the ball is outside `.h5-xterm` (so the terminal's listeners never see the touch), it takes the pointer capture, has `touch-action: none`, no text selection or callout, and cancels `touchstart` / `touchmove` / `touchend` and `contextmenu` (non-passive listeners). One finger drives; a second one is ignored. A terminal that goes away during the touch (another session, the chat) ends it.
- **Accessibility.** The ball is `role="button"` with `aria-label` and `title` "Mouse ball" (Chinese in `i18n-zh-ui.js`) and `aria-pressed`; it is not focusable: it is a touch tool, and keyboard users select with the keyboard and the copy view.
- **Limits** (not in this version): only the left button; no modifiers (Shift or Option to force the page selection over a program, as a desktop mouse can). A program that reports the mouse but does not select or copy on its own gets the clicks and drags and nothing is copied; turn its mouse mode off (or use the copy view) to copy in the page. A click without selection (a tap at the cursor) does nothing. No horizontal scrolling while selecting in Wide mode (the cursor is kept inside the visible cells). The copy on lift relies on `pointerup` counting as a user activation on iOS Safari; where it does not, the refusal bar finishes the copy. Not tried on a real phone (only headless Chromium with CDP touches) and not with a real Claude Code fullscreen session (the test plays the program with SGR drag tracking and an OSC 52 reply).
- **Tests.** `test/mouse-ball-model.test.js` (the offset and its edge fade, the clamp and the last column, the dock, the grab fade, the cell under a point in a scrolled view, the selection over one and several rows in both directions, the dwell reducer with a fake clock, the scroll speed and step, the button mode and what each tracking mode is sent); `scripts/test-h5-mouse-ball-browser.cjs` (390 x 844, real CDP touches, a mocked server: off by default and its chunk not loaded, the More menu switch after Terminal width, remembered, the dock place above the key rows, the cursor 46px up and left with the dashed line, the ring and the press after 600 ms without opening the copy view, the selection drawn, the lift copying exactly the expected text with the notice, docking back, the edge fade reaching the last column, a drag before the dwell and a cancelled touch copying nothing, the terminal not scrolling under the ball, a held button near the top edge scrolling with the selection keeping its start, the refusal bar; with SGR drag tracking on: nothing sent before the press, the press at the dwell, motions while held and the release at the lift with the right 1-based cells, no page selection or copy, the program's OSC 52 after the lift written at once (and the request bar without a recent gesture), wheel-up reports near the top edge without moving the page view, reporting turned off mid-gesture keeping the gesture the program's, then the page selection and copy working again; no horizontal overflow, off again after a reload); `test/page-bundles.test.js` (the chunk, the first-screen budget).
